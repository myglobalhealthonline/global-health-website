Where the site stands

The latest complete Google Search Console week, 7 to 13 August, recorded 204 clicks and 16,769 impressions. The prior week recorded 172 clicks and 7,399 impressions. Clicks rose 19% and impressions rose 127%.

Average position moved from 17.8 to 19.1 because 387 additional pages gained visibility, led by tools, blogs and newly ranking lab pages. The 14-day click total is flat and the 28-day total is up 72%. Five of six markets gained clicks; Spain was effectively flat at 24 versus 25.

If you only do one thing

Request a fresh Google crawl for the Ireland neurology consultation page.

This page moved from 26 impressions, 2 clicks and position 15.5 to 3 impressions, no clicks and position 30.3. Its live page is healthy, but Google's stored copy dates from 18 July and does not record the page's current preferred address. The old crawl is not proven to have caused the loss; refreshing it is a low-risk way to remove that uncertainty.

  1. Open Google Search Console and choose the sc-domain:myglobalhealth.online property.
  2. Inspect https://www.myglobalhealth.online/ireland/en/services/neurology-specialist-consultation, then select Request indexing.
  3. Recheck the page after seven complete days. Treat recovery toward at least 15 impressions or position 20 as improvement; investigate its exact queries and live competitors if the loss persists for 14 days.

Small fixes, in order

Track stable money pages separately high

The sitewide average mixes current commercial pages with thousands of new, low-click tool and information impressions. Mobile position was stable at 12.4 to 12.6 while desktop visibility more than doubled and entered deeper results.

Implemented: The admin Search Console report now separates the stable revenue-relevant pages, home, services, doctors and labs, from /tools/, informational blogs, legacy URLs and other pages. It also uses authoritative totals and defaults to 28 likely-complete days, ending three days before the request date.

Watch homepage clicks, not rank medium

The homepage fell from 35 to 17 clicks while impressions stayed at 429 versus 427. Its average position improved from 24.6 to 18.2, so this is a click-through or query-mix change rather than a ranking loss.

Fix: Keep the current title for one more complete week. If clicks remain below 20 with similar impressions, compare branded queries and the live search snippet before testing a title that includes the Global Health brand.

Keep the Spanish doctor page live medium

/spain/es/doctors/dr-alfredo-del-valle lost 25 impressions and 4 clicks in the latest week, but Google reports the page is submitted and indexed, Google's preferred address matches the site's, and the live page returns 200 with index, follow.

Fix: Make no code change. Recheck the clinician-name query after another complete week; the current sample is too small to separate demand variation from a ranking change.

Record sitemap growth low

The live sitemap has 1,932 URLs, up from 1,906 on 12 August. Eight sampled entries were indexable, self-canonical and returned 200. There were 314 entries without a modification date, nine more than the last ledger check.

Fix: Update the baseline only. Do not invent modification dates for static pages; use real source dates when they exist.

Treat ledger sweep mismatches as historical rows low

The sweep reported three disagreements. Two are explicitly labelled pre-retirement FAQ observations and the third is a historical Spanish-doctor observation. The live behavior matches the later ledger decisions.

Fix: Keep the dated evidence, but teach the sweep to ignore tables marked superseded during its next maintenance pass.
