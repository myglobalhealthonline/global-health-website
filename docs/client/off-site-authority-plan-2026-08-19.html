Six of these links come from pages that sell backlinks, and they name us in the advertisement.

The anchor text on m98ufa.com, betulcrime.com, betwinnermirror.com, plumeriamarketing.com, ggmap.us.com and homesforsaleoldgreenwichct.com reads, word for word:

“High Quality Dofollow Backlinks DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN Network Service myglobalhealth.online Rank First Page Google Fast SEO Link Building Buy Backlinks Online Cheap”

Between them, these six sites score 60–75 out of 100 for spam. Alongside them, the number of linking sites jumped from 42 to 53 in May 2026 alone, almost entirely article-posting sites.

The one question we need answered before anything else: did anyone buy a link package, or hire an agency that did? Not a problem to admit — it changes what we do next, and guessing wrong is expensive either way.

Do not disavow anything yet. Disavowing tells Google to ignore links. Google already ignores most low-quality links on its own, and a disavow file that is too broad throws away good links with the bad ones — permanently, and it takes months to recover.

We only file one if Google Search Console shows a manual action against the site. It currently does not.

Where to get listed, by country

Regulator registers and professional bodies first — they are free, they are permanent, and they are the only links a competitor cannot outspend us on. Health directories second, for patient traffic as much as ranking.

Two of these are compliance obligations, not marketing. The Portuguese ERS register and the Czech national provider register are where a licensed healthcare provider is supposed to appear. Confirming the Global Health entry exists and is current is worth doing regardless of what it does for search.

Wikidata — free, and half-done already

Global Health has a Wikidata entry, and it is in better shape than most. Wikidata is the structured database Google and the AI assistants read to work out what a company is, so gaps there show up as gaps in how the brand is understood.

Already on the entry: official website, headquarters, founding year, legal name, employee count, the six operating countries, industry, founder, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, four Facebook pages, five Instagram accounts, and a photo.

Worth adding

  • The YouTube channel — @GlobalHealth-y9o
  • The TikTok account — globalhealth.online
  • The company logo, as a separate item from the existing photo
  • The public contact email and phone number
  • The Czech company registration number (IČO) for Global Guest s.r.o.

Anyone can edit Wikidata with a free account. If it would be easier, we can prepare the exact entries for someone to paste in.

Do not try to create a Wikipedia article. Wikipedia requires substantial coverage in independent publications that Global Health does not have yet. An article created too early gets deleted, and a deletion discussion is a permanent, publicly searchable record. Worth revisiting after real press coverage exists — which is part of why the Irish tech-press item above is on the list.

Three things not to do

  1. Do not buy more links. Whatever the last campaign cost, the ranking has not moved in twelve months. Six of those links are now sitting on pages advertising link-selling services with our domain name in the sales copy.
  2. Do not ask the article sites to remove their links. It gains nothing, and some of them respond by demanding payment.
  3. Do not file a disavow file. Not unless Google Search Console shows a manual action. It is easy to do harm and slow to undo.
