Driving licence medical certificate in Portugal

Your category, age and professional use determine the requirements. An appointment does not guarantee that a certificate can be issued.

Group 1 covers motorcycles, mopeds, light vehicles and agricultural tractors. Group 2 covers heavy vehicles and certain professional uses of B or BE. The doctor submits the certificate to IMT, but cannot guarantee its issuance. Some cases need an in-person assessment, further evidence or a psychological certificate.

Which categories belong to Group 1 and Group 2?

Check your category and vehicle use before booking.

  • Group 1: AM, A1, A2, A, B1, B and BE categories, mopeds and agricultural tractors.
  • Group 2 by category: C1, C1E, C, CE, D1, D1E, D and DE.
  • Professional B or BE: ambulances, fire-service vehicles, patient transport, school or child transport, and hire cars used for passenger transport fall under Group 2.
  • If you are unsure: check the category and describe the driving activity. A B or BE licence does not always mean Group 1.

The group changes the medical criteria, renewal intervals and additional assessments.

Renewal

When are medical and psychological certificates required?

Deadlines depend on category, acquisition date and age.

The Justice portal requires a medical certificate from age 60 for Group 1 and a psychological certificate after 70. Group 2 renewals require a medical certificate and, after 50, a psychological certificate. First licences, foreign-licence exchanges and health-authority directions may follow different rules.

The psychotechnical test must be completed in person. When required, Global Health refers the patient to a trusted partner clinic and coordinates the next steps.

Assessment

What to prepare for the medical assessment

The appointment provides the evidence for a clinical decision.

Bring identification, your category, medication list and relevant reports. The doctor may need to check vision, mobility, chronic illness, loss of consciousness or treatment affecting alertness.

  • Your driving licence, category and any notice from IMT.
  • Your medication, relevant medical reports, glasses or contact lenses.

An online appointment may suffice. Otherwise, the doctor will request an in-person examination, test or additional opinion.

No guarantee before assessmentThe appointment pays for an assessment, not an outcome. Issuance requires sufficient evidence and compliance with the rules.

If the certificate cannot be issued, ask whether you need a report, examination, vision check, specialist opinion or psychological assessment before renewal.

Electronic route

How electronic submission to IMT works

Once issued, the doctor submits the certificate electronically to IMT.

You need not send a copy. Renewal is a separate online or in-person application. Add a psychological certificate or other document when required.

Keep the renewal confirmation and respond to IMT requests.

If the certificate is missing, contact the issuer and IMT. The clinic does not decide the renewal.

FAQ

Common questions

Which licence categories belong to Group 1 and Group 2?

Group 1 covers AM, A1, A2, A, B1, B, BE, mopeds and agricultural tractors. Group 2 covers C1, C1E, C, CE, D1, D1E, D, DE and specified professional uses of B or BE.

Does online renewal remove the need for a medical appointment?

No. The application can be online, but the certificate requires medical assessment. The doctor submits it electronically to IMT after issuance.

When is a psychological fitness certificate required?

The Justice portal requires it after 70 for Group 1 and after 50 for Group 2 renewals. Check with IMT.

Does an online appointment guarantee the certificate?

No. It may suffice, but the doctor can request reports, an in-person assessment or another opinion, or find that the criteria are not met.
