Clinic networks, doctor marketplaces, public-health authorities, insurers, editorial publishers, and focused pediatric/consultation products all compete for the same journeys. ## Seed deep dives

### DoktorOnline

The name is an exact-category match, but OpenSEO estimates only one Czech ranking keyword and three visits. It has 191 filtered referring domains with a spam score of 58, so raw link count is not a quality advantage. Treat it as a positioning reference, not the current SEO benchmark.

### uLékaře

Its moat is editorial breadth: conditions, patient questions, advisory content, and strong topical familiarity. It is not a like-for-like virtual GP journey, but it dominates informational discovery. Global Health should not copy its breadth; it should connect fewer, clinically reviewed explainers directly to bookable services.

### MEDDI

MEDDI is the direct app-led comparator: speed, multi-channel contact, employer/subscription contexts, and recognizable telemedicine framing. Its estimated Czech search footprint is smaller than major publishers, but it regularly appears for high-value online-doctor terms. Global Health can differentiate with web-first accessibility, named doctors, transparent scope, and no unsupported immediacy promise.

### EUC / mojeEUC

EUC combines clinic-network authority with “Můj praktik online”. Its digital offer is partly gated to registered/existing patients, while the larger domain wins through institutional pages. Global Health’s opening is a clearer standalone online-consultation proposition, explicit eligibility, and a simpler booking path.

### ZnámýLékař

The marketplace scales through roughly 49,000 specialists, profiles, reviews, locations, availability, and visible price inventory. It is a discovery platform, not a single care provider. Global Health should compete on service certainty, clinical governance, and a coherent end-to-end visit—not attempt marketplace-style page multiplication.

### Canadian Medical

Canadian Medical combines a premium Prague clinic reputation with a 24/7 virtual clinic and strong English/expat relevance. Access is membership/eligibility-led and current retail pricing was not clearly visible during review. Global Health can win selected expat queries with transparent pricing, web booking, and clear online-versus-in-person boundaries.

## Automatically discovered competitors

- `konzultacelekare.cz`: named physicians, clean transactional funnel, and prices such as 670 CZK observed on reviewed pages; less institutional authority.
- `mojeambulance.cz`: frequent GP/clinic result and local-care authority.
- `zpmvcr.cz`, `nzip.cz`, `cssz.cz`: insurer/government information competitors for sickness, referral, and health-system queries.
- `epediatr.cz`: focused pediatric transaction competitor.
- `medevio.cz`: digital-care workflow competitor.
- `praktickylekar.online`: HCP-only publication; exclude as consumer product comparator but retain in SERP evidence.

## What Global Health can credibly own

1. One clear Czech commercial owner for online GP consultation.
2. A transactional neschopenka page paired with an official-source explainer.
3. Prague and English-speaking care for foreigners, supported by existing GSC demand.
4. Transparent eligibility, price, named doctor, limitations, and escalation language.
5. Focused pediatric, dermatology, treatment-renewal, referral, and second-opinion paths where the service already exists.
