## DrOnline

DrOnline is the strongest direct consumer competitor in the sample. Its 689 ranked terms show dedicated ownership of high-intent certificate and teleconsultation themes. A live result for `atestado médico` placed its atestados page at position 2 in the provider snapshot. Its product architecture uses exact service landing pages and concise transaction-first messaging.

Backlink overview: 600 backlinks, 516 referring domains, rank 25, target spam score 0. The unusually high referring-domain/backlink ratio should be validated at row level before using it as a quality benchmark. Strong observed referring sources include SAPO and Portuguese directory/editorial domains; low-value catalogs should not be copied.

Actionable lesson: compete where Global Health has verified supply, but do not clone DrOnline’s certificate wording. For driving certificates, the Global Health page is indexed yet still around position 35–40; the gap is authority/SERP competitiveness, not a demonstrated title bug.

## Knok

Knok’s bounded Portugal footprint is smaller: 33 ranked keywords, estimated traffic 639. Its positioning is more platform/B2B-led than a pure consumer service-page network. Backlink overview: 152 backlinks, 60 referring domains, rank 24, target spam score 13. One large source in the overview was suspicious/low-relevance, so gross backlink totals are not an outreach template.

Actionable lesson: Knok is relevant for telehealth entity/technology visibility, but DrOnline, Médico na Net and Teleconsulta Portugal are closer page-level benchmarks for immediate consumer intent.

## Other competitors

- [Médico na Net](https://www.mediconanet.pt/) ranks across online doctor, prescription, certificate and consultation themes.
- [Teleconsulta Portugal](https://teleconsultaportugal.com/) uses a narrow teleconsultation architecture and appeared prominently for generic consultation terms.
- [SNS 24](https://www.sns24.gov.pt/) dominates public-process intent. Those queries belong in informational/comparison handling, not Global Health service claims.
- [CUF](https://www.cuf.pt/), [Hospital da Luz](https://www.hospitaldaluz.pt/), and [Lusíadas](https://www.lusiadas.pt/) win through institutional authority, locations and specialty depth. Do not create false local clinic pages to imitate them.
- [Médis](https://www.medis.pt/) and [Multicare](https://medicinaonline.multicare.pt/) combine insurance membership and app access; their commercial model differs from direct pay.
- Consultas Online has limited domain scale (13 backlinks, two referring domains in the overview) and is not an authority benchmark.

## SERP validation

Ten queries × 20 rows are preserved in `serp-validation.csv`. Priority SERPs included `médico online`, `consulta médica online`, `teleconsulta`, `consulta online`, `médico de família online`, `atestado médico online`, `baixa médica online`, `receita médica online`, `atestado médico para carta`, and `consulta do viajante online`.

Observed patterns:

- generic online-doctor SERPs mix digital providers, insurers, public health and hospital networks;
- certificate/leave/prescription SERPs are sensitive to Portuguese rules and official-domain dominance;
- Brazilian results can intrude into Portuguese-language SERPs and were excluded from target mapping;
- SERP features include AI overviews, People Also Ask, images, related searches and knowledge panels;
- exact-service pages outperform broad market pages for transaction-specific terms.

## Keyword/page gaps

The cleaned gap export contains 177 rows where a sampled competitor ranks and Global Health is absent or below position 10 after service/business gating. The most defensible work is optimization of existing consultation, traveller-health, mental-health and staffed specialty pages. Prescription topics require clinical/compliance approval. Driving-certificate terms remain a long-term authority project despite high volume.

No competitor evidence justifies hundreds of new pages. Every approved cluster maps to an existing URL.
