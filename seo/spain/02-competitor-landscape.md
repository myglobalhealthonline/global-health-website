rank 15. The old blanket claim that it is absent from the top 20 is therefore stale.

Health is absent from that sampled top 20. Spanish dermatology queries still reach
the English service in GSC, so intent/locale ownership remains an investigation hold.

medical assessment only; do not turn state sick-leave demand into a commercial
promise. BOE RD 625/2014 article 2 identifies the public health service/mutua route.
