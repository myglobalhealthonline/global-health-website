From anywhere. Anytime.About UsGlobal Health is a telemedicine platform already operating in Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania, Czechia, and Brazil. Our promise is simple: Medicine Anytime, Anywhere. We're building a clinic that removes barriers between doctors and the patients who need them — wherever they are.Why Online Gynaecology Is Needed in Ireland Right NowGynaecology services in Ireland are under significant pressure from high demand — the HSE itself has described much of this as previously unmet or hidden need finally surfacing. The government's 2026 Waiting Time Action Plan and the National Endometriosis Framework both aim to improve access, but waiting lists continue to be affected by surges in referrals and seasonal capacity constraints.The Irish market for online women's health has grown quickly around specific needs like contraception and menopause management, with several platforms already offering repeat prescriptions and basic consultations in this space. What's less common is accessible online access to a full specialist gynaecologist for more complex concerns — fertility support, endometriosis assessment, and ongoing gynaecological follow-up — which is where a genuinely specialist-led offering can stand apart.Online gynaecology is one real way to help meet this demand — and you can be part of it.The Position: Gynaecologist for Online ConsultationsWe're looking for licensed gynaecologists based in Ireland to join our online clinic. Through our platform, you'll provide remote gynaecology consultations to patients across the country, with full flexibility to shape your work schedule.
100% remote — consult from anywhere, via video, with access to patient history and test results
Flexible schedule — choose the hours and days that suit you
Main or supplementary role — ideal alongside an existing practice or hospital position
Clear referral protocols — defined pathways for cases requiring in-person or urgent care (e.g. significant bleeding, acute pelvic pain)
E-prescribing fully integrated — including contraception and hormone therapy, no extra paperwork
What You'll Be Part Of
A genuinely international team of doctors — collaborate and connect with colleagues across Europe and Brazil
A growing platform — you're joining Global Health at a growth stage in Ireland, not a position within an already-established system
Real impact — help patients access regular gynaecological care, including contraception, menopause management, and fertility support, without long waits
Ongoing support — technical, administrative, and clinical support so you can focus on medicine, not paperwork
Who We're Looking For
Valid medical registration in Ireland (Irish Medical Council)
Specialisation in Obstetrics and Gynaecology
Experience with telemedicine tools is a plus (training and onboarding support provided)
Fluent English; Portuguese, Spanish, Romanian, or Czech are a bonus given our presence in those markets
Why NowWith gynaecology services under real pressure and a national framework now dedicated specifically to conditions like endometriosis, the demand for accessible, specialist-led online gynaecology in Ireland is real and growing. Doctors who join at this stage help shape how online gynaecology actually works within Global Health in Ireland — this isn't just about filling a schedule, it's about building something new.
