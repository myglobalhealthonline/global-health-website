Global Health is a telemedicine platform launching in Czechia, part of a growing network already connecting patients and doctors across Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania and Brazil. Our promise is simple: Medicine Anytime, Anywhere. We are building a clinic that removes barriers between doctors and the patients who need them, wherever they may be.
The role
We are looking for licensed general practitioners working in Czechia to join our online clinic as we launch locally. You will provide remote consultations to patients through our platform, with the flexibility to shape your working pattern around your needs.
100% remote — consult from anywhereFlexible schedule — choose the hours and days that suit youChoose how you work: DPP (Agreement to Perform Work) or self-employed (OSVČ)Both full-time and part-time arrangements are considered
What you will be part of
A genuinely international medical team — collaborate and connect with colleagues across Europe and BrazilA platform with room to grow — you are joining Global Health at its founding stage in Czechia, not stepping into an established systemMeaningful impact — help patients who might otherwise struggle to access timely care, from wherever you workOngoing support — technical, administrative and clinical support so you can focus on medicine rather than paperwork
Who we are looking for
A valid medical licence in Czechia, registered with the Czech Medical Chamber (ČLK)Qualification or specialisation in general practiceExperience with telemedicine tools is an advantage; training and onboarding support are providedFluent Czech; English is helpful for working with our international team. Portuguese, Spanish or Romanian are also welcome given our presence in those markets
Why now
We are actively growing across six markets, and Czechia is a priority. Doctors who join at this stage will help shape how the clinic works locally — this is more than simply filling a schedule.
