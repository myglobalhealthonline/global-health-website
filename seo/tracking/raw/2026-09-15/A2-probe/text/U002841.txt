Most hair loss conditions are assessed and managed at primary-care level, including male and female androgenetic alopecia, telogen effluvium, nutritional-deficiency-related shedding, and mild alopecia areata. Complex or scarring conditions are referred to dermatology. Your GP is the right point of contact for an initial hair loss assessment.Do I need to have tests done before the consultation?No. Your doctor assesses during the consultation whether tests are indicated and, if so, issues the request on the same day. Bring any recent test results, if you have them.Can hair loss be reversed?It depends on the type and how long it has been progressing. Androgenetic alopecia — in men and women — responds best to treatment started early, while follicles are thinning rather than already lost. Telogen effluvium is, as a rule, self-limiting and resolves once the underlying cause is treated. Nutritional-deficiency-related hair loss responds well to correcting the deficiency. Scarring alopecia requires urgent specialist assessment, as it can cause permanent loss if untreated.Can women use this service for hair loss?Yes. Hair loss in women is a core part of this service. The clinical picture in women is often more complex than male androgenetic alopecia — involving hormonal, nutritional, and autoimmune factors — and benefits significantly from structured clinical assessment.What if my hair loss is related to medication I'm taking?Medication-related hair loss is assessed as part of this consultation. Your doctor reviews your current medication, identifies whether any of it is contributing to the hair loss, and advises on appropriate management — including whether adjusting medication is clinically appropriate in the context of your overall health.Can I use this service if I have already tried over-the-counter treatments?Yes. Many patients have already tried topical minoxidil or other over-the-counter options before seeking clinical assessment. Bring information about what you have already tried and your assessment of the response — this is clinically useful information your doctor incorporates into the management plan.From what age is this service suitable?Hair loss can begin in a person's twenties, in both men and women. This service is suitable for any adult with hair loss or thinning hair, regardless of age.
