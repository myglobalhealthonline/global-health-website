An inspection can arrive at any time, even on a Saturday evening.Outings during sick leave are authorised by your treating doctor, for at most 6 hours a day, between 7:00 and 19:00. Outside those hours, you must be at the address you reported to your doctor. Your employer can check on you during the first 14 days, and the OSSZ can check at any time throughout your sick leave, regardless of the hour. The law has no such thing as "unlimited outings"; there is only one narrow exception, and it requires the OSSZ's consent. If you do get them, they can be a single block (say, 13:00 to 19:00) or split up. They apply every day, including weekends, and they don't carry over: if you don't go out on Monday, you don't get twelve hours on Tuesday.Maximum: 6 hours a day, always between 7:00 and 19:00.Who decides: your treating doctor, by entering it in eNeschopenka.Where outings apply: the law doesn't limit the distance from your address, only the time. A trip to the doctor or to a medical examination counts as part of your treatment regimen.Outside outing hours: you're at your reported address. It doesn't have to be your permanent residence, but it must be stated on your sick leave certificate.Rules verified on 7 September 2026 against § 56 zákona č. 187/2006 Sb. and the ČSSZ – Illness Made Simple page.What the law doesn't recogniseIs there such a thing as unlimited outings?Not in the sense people mean in online discussions. The law has just one exception, and it's a narrow one.Under the second sentence of § 56 odst. 6, in exceptional cases your doctor may allow you to choose your outing hours according to your current state of health. This applies to an unusually demanding treatment plan, ongoing intensive treatment, adverse side effects of treatment, or an overall serious condition. It's typically oncology patients or people recovering from major surgery.Two conditions still apply: you request it and your doctor agrees, and the OSSZ gives its prior written consent. Without that, the arrangement doesn't apply. The six-hour limit between 7:00 and 19:00 still applies; you're only choosing when within the day you use it.Be wary of advice from the internetGuides on "how to get unlimited outings" are usually describing exactly this exception, just without the conditions attached. If you don't meet the medical grounds, your doctor cannot grant it and the OSSZ will not approve it.Your employer and the OSSZWho checks on you and when they can comeDuring the first 14 days, your employer can check on you. The OSSZ can come at any time for the whole duration of your sick leave.During the first 14 calendar days, your employer pays your wage compensation, and under § 192 zákoníku práce it therefore has the right to verify that you're home and observing your outing hours. It only checks your whereabouts and compliance with the outing hours, not your treatment. If it finds a breach, it draws up a record and sends it to you, the OSSZ, and your doctor.The OSSZ checks your entire regimen, including the treatment side, from day one until your sick leave ends. Asked whether an inspection can come in the evening or at the weekend, the ČSSZ answers directly: the possibility of an inspection is not limited to normal working hours, and an inspector can come even at the weekend or outside working hours. So the question of "an inspection after 10 p.m." has no limit set in law. If your outing hours end at 19:00, then at 22:00 you're simply meant to be home.Under § 64 zákona o nemocenském pojištění, you must allow the inspection to take place and cooperate, meaning you must prove your identity.A broken doorbell or a missing nameplate count against you, because labelling your home with your name is your legal obligation.Source: ČSSZ's most frequent questions on temporary incapacity for work, § 192 zákoníku práce and ČSSZ Sick Pay (policyholder obligations), accessed 7 September 2026.What the OSSZ doesThe inspection came and you weren't homeFirst comes a written request, not a penalty. What matters is how quickly and how credibly you respond.According to the ČSSZ, if OSSZ staff don't find you at home, they leave a written notice asking you to get in touch. The OSSZ assesses the reasons for your absence and only then, if warranted, opens administrative proceedings. A doctor's visit, a hospital examination, or hospitalisation are reasons you can document. "I'd just gone out for bread rolls" outside your permitted hours isn't a valid reason.Get in touch as soon as possible, ideally the same day, and have proof at hand: a medical report, a referral, a discharge summary. If you were home and simply didn't hear the doorbell, say so straight away, and check whether the doorbell works and carries your name.What happens if a breach is confirmedThe OSSZ can reduce or withdraw your sick pay from the day of the breach, for up to 100 calendar days. A breach of the rules, or refusing to cooperate, can carry a fine of up to CZK 20,000. Your employer can reduce or withhold your wage compensation during the first 14 days, and for a particularly serious breach can dismiss you within one month of discovering it.§ 125 a § 128 zákona o nemocenském pojištění, SÚIP's guidance on § 301a zákoníku práce and the ČSSZ Sick Pay page.Practical situationsHow to change your address, your outing hours, or go stay with your parentsEverything goes through your treating doctor, and always in advance.Changing address: ask your doctor in advance, and they'll enter it in eNeschopenka. During the first 14 days, also notify your employer of the change.Changing your outing hours: same process, through your doctor. It can't be done retroactively; an inspection compares reality with what was recorded on the day of the visit.Staying abroad: requires the OSSZ's prior consent, not just your doctor's.Hospitalisation or a spa stay: on admission to inpatient care, no change needs to be requested. After discharge, tell your doctor where you're staying.The law makes no explicit mention of dogs or gardens. The general rule applies: you may only be outside your home during permitted hours, and those start no earlier than 7:00. If you need to go out earlier, arrange your hours with your doctor accordingly.§ 56 odst. 3 zákona č. 187/2006 Sb.
