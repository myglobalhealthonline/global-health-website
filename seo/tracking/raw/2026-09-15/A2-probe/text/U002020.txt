This allows adequate time for vaccine administration and for multi-dose vaccines to be completed before travel. Your doctor will advise on which destinations require yellow fever certification and direct you to the nearest designated centre in Ireland.Can you administer vaccines during the consultation?No. This is a clinical assessment and planning service. Vaccine administration requires in-person attendance. Your doctor will advise on which vaccines are recommended and where to attend for administration.Can I use this service if I am travelling with children?Yes. Travel health consultations for families with children are appropriate through this service for older children and adolescents. For very young infants, travel health advice should be sought through our Paediatric GP Consultation in the first instance.What should I do if I become unwell while abroad?Seek local medical attention for any urgent concern. For non-urgent concerns, you can access our service remotely while abroad. Your doctor can assess your symptoms via video call and advise on appropriate management or escalation. For medical emergencies abroad, contact local emergency services and your travel insurer immediately.Can you provide documentation for medications I am carrying?Yes.
