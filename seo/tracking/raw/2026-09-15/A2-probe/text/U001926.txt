From anywhere. Anytime.About UsGlobal Health is a telemedicine platform already operating in Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania, Czechia, and Brazil. Our promise is simple: Medicine Anytime, Anywhere. We're building a clinic that removes barriers between doctors and the patients who need them — wherever they are.Why Online Endocrinology Is Needed in Ireland Right NowA 2026 government report confirmed significant staffing gaps across Ireland's diabetes and endocrinology teams, with severe regional disparities. In HSE West and North West acute hospitals, for example, there are just 3 whole-time equivalent endocrinologists dedicated to diabetes care against a target of 15.5 — a gap that leaves many patients without timely access to specialist follow-up.There's real evidence that alternative care models can help close this gap: a community-based diabetes hub pilot in Dublin North West reduced hospital waiting times for diabetes appointments by 61% in its first year. Endocrinology conditions — diabetes, thyroid disorders, weight management, hormonal imbalances — typically require ongoing, regular follow-up rather than one-off visits, which is exactly the kind of care that adapts well to a remote consultation model.Online endocrinology is one real way to help close this gap — and you can be part of it.The Position: Endocrinologist for Online ConsultationsWe're looking for licensed endocrinologists based in Ireland to join our online clinic. Through our platform, you'll provide remote endocrinology consultations to patients across the country, with full flexibility to shape your work schedule.
100% remote — consult from anywhere, via video, with access to patient labs and history
Flexible schedule — choose the hours and days that suit you
Main or supplementary role — ideal alongside an existing practice or hospital position
E-prescribing fully integrated — including chronic medication renewal, no extra paperwork
What You'll Be Part Of
A genuinely international team of doctors — collaborate and connect with colleagues across Europe and Brazil
A growing platform — you're joining Global Health at a growth stage in Ireland, not a position within an already-established system
Real impact — help patients with diabetes, thyroid conditions, and weight management get the regular follow-up they need, without long waits
Ongoing support — technical, administrative, and clinical support so you can focus on medicine, not paperwork
Who We're Looking For
Valid medical registration in Ireland (Irish Medical Council)
Specialisation in Endocrinology and Diabetes
Experience with telemedicine tools is a plus (training and onboarding support provided)
Fluent English; Portuguese, Spanish, Romanian, or Czech are a bonus given our presence in those markets
Why NowWith some regions running at under a fifth of their target endocrinology staffing, and evidence that alternative care models can cut waiting times significantly, the case for accessible online endocrinology in Ireland is clear. Doctors who join at this stage help shape how online endocrinology actually works within Global Health in Ireland — this isn't just about filling a schedule, it's about building something new.
