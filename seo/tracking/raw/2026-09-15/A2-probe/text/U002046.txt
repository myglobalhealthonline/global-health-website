The window as a whole covers about six days: the five before ovulation plus the day itself. Sperm survive up to five days and the egg under one, which is why the window opens early and closes fast.Does ovulation always happen on day 14?No. Day 14 is only true of a textbook 28-day cycle. Ovulation sits roughly 14 days before your next period, so on a 32-day cycle it lands around day 18 and on a 24-day cycle around day 10. Counting 14 days forward from your last period is the commonest way to get the timing wrong.How do I work out ovulation with irregular cycles?Enter your usual cycle length and treat the result as a wide estimate rather than a date. If your cycles vary by more than a few days, ovulation predictor kits or tracking cervical mucus will tell you far more than any calendar calculation, and cycles that are consistently under 21 or over 35 days are worth a doctor's opinion.Can I use an ovulation calculator as contraception?No. It is not a contraceptive method and should never be used as one. Ovulation shifts between cycles, sperm can survive up to five days, and pregnancy is possible on days this calculator marks as outside the fertile window. If you are avoiding pregnancy, use a recognised method of contraception.When can I take a pregnancy test after ovulation?Most home tests are reliable from the day your period is due, which is about 14 days after ovulation — the "test from" date in the read-out. Testing earlier can give a negative result even when you are pregnant, because there is not yet enough hCG in urine to detect.Does this ovulation calculator store my details?No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to our servers or saved.Where do I get fertility advice in Ireland?Start with a GP. They can check the basics: cycles, thyroid, rubella immunity and bloods for both partners, then refer on to a fertility service when that is the next step. Our Irish-registered doctors can go through irregular cycles and preconception checks by video first, which is often enough to know whether a referral is what you need.Can I buy ovulation tests in Ireland?Yes. Ovulation predictor kits are sold over the counter in Irish pharmacies and supermarkets, with no prescription needed. They detect the LH surge 24 to 36 hours before the egg is released, so start testing a few days before the date this calculator estimates.Trying to conceive?Talk to a registered doctor by video about irregular cycles, preconception checks or the medicines you already take.Book a consultation
