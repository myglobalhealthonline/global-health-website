Understanding the Difference from a Medical CertificateThese are two documents with different purposes. One justifies the time you spent at the appointment; the other justifies the day you were unable to work.The attendance certificate, also called an attendance declaration, proves that you attended an appointment, test or therapy session on a given day and shift. It does not excuse the entire day by law: according to the Tribunal Superior do Trabalho, it justifies only the time of the appointment, and the rest depends on company policy or the collective bargaining agreement. What excuses the absence is the medical certificate, which only a doctor can sign and which states that you were unfit for work, based on Lei 605/1949. The CLT guarantees specific absences, such as up to 3 days a year for cancer preventive screening.Attendance certificate: justifies the shift of the appointment, not the dayMedical certificate: excuses the absence for proven illness (Lei 605/1949)CLT art. 473: up to 3 days/year for cancer preventive screeningTST's explanation of the two documentsOnline medical certificate: validityWhat this guide coversWhich document to request when leaving the appointment, depending on how you feel.The three situations where the CLT guarantees the absence even without illness.What a doctor can and cannot write, including about the CID.GHGlobal Health Medical TeamReviewed by the Global Health medical team.Two documents, two purposesAttendance certificate and medical certificate: what's the differenceThe two names sound similar, but each document states something different.Attendance declaration (or certificate): states that you were at the location, on a given date and shift, for an appointment, test, therapy session or to accompany someone. It can be signed by a doctor, dentist, psychologist, physiotherapist or the facility's front desk.Medical certificate: states that you were unfit for work for a specific period. Only a doctor can sign it, and the document must include the day, the time and the required rest period.The TST sums it up this way: both justify health-related absences, but only the medical certificate covers the entire day. The attendance certificate covers only the time you needed to be away for the appointment. The TRT da 18ª Região ruled the same way, denying validity as a medical certificate to an attendance certificate issued by a health clinic.TST — Do you know the difference between a medical certificate and an attendance certificate? and TRT-18. Accessed September 7, 2026.What the law guaranteesDoes the attendance certificate excuse the absence?By law, only the time of the appointment. The rest of the day depends on the company or the collective bargaining agreement.Lei 605/1949 states that an employee's illness, proven by a certificate, is a justified and paid absence. It says nothing about attending an appointment. If you went to the doctor at 9 a.m. and returned at 11 a.m., the certificate justifies those two hours. If you stayed home the whole day with only that certificate, the company can deduct the rest, unless a collective agreement or internal policy is more favorable. Some companies excuse the whole day as a matter of their own policy; check yours. There is no legal limit on the number of certificates, as long as they are genuine and issued by a qualified professional, as the TST has already clarified.I got the attendance certificate and didn't go back to workIf you left the appointment unfit for work, the correct document is the medical certificate, and it is up to the doctor to assess that. Staying home with only the attendance certificate, when the doctor did not certify incapacity, can be treated as a partial absence.Lei 605/1949, art. 6º, §1º, f and TST — Is there a legal limit on attendance certificates?.CLT, art. 473When the CLT guarantees the absence even without being illThree health-related situations are set out in law and don't depend on the company's goodwill. The attendance certificate is what proves each of them.Cancer preventive screening: up to 3 days every 12 months of work, duly proven (inciso XII, Lei 13.767/2018). Since April 2026, Lei 15.377 requires the employer to inform the employee about this option, expressly mentioning HPV and cancer screening tests.Accompanying a wife or partner during pregnancy: for the time needed for up to 6 medical appointments or complementary tests (inciso X, redação da Lei 14.457/2022).Accompanying a child up to age 6 to a medical appointment: 1 day per year (inciso XI, Lei 13.257/2016).Your own appointment, test or therapy, outside these situations: there is no legal guarantee of excused absence; the shift-based rule explained above applies.In these cases, a medical certificate is not required. It is the attendance certificate, with the date, shift and purpose, that proves the reason set out in law.Lei 13.767/2018, Lei 15.377/2026 and CLT, art. 473, incisos X e XI.Minimum contentWhat the attendance certificate must includeWithout these items, the company has grounds to refuse it. Check before you leave the front desk.The patient's full name (or the accompanied person's name, in the case of a companion).The date and shift of the appointment, or the arrival and departure times.The location of the appointment.Purpose: appointment, test, therapy or accompanying a patient.The professional's signature and identification, with a stamp or professional council registration number.If the document is issued by a doctor, the CFM rules apply: legible details, signature and stamp or CRM number, and the time on record. The CID is only included with your express authorization (Resolução CFM 1.658/2002, art. 5º). The company can check the document's authenticity, not demand the diagnosis.TST and Resolução CFM 1.658/2002, arts. 3º a 5º.In-person or online appointmentWhat the doctor can write, including for an online appointmentThe doctor certifies what they observed. They cannot issue a document without a medical act to justify it, and cannot refuse to certify what they did.It's set out in the Código de Ética Médica: issuing a document without a professional act to justify it is prohibited (art. 80), and so is refusing to certify acts already performed when the patient requests it (art. 91). At the end of any appointment, you are entitled to the attendance certificate. Whether a certificate with time off is issued depends on the assessment: if the doctor determines that you are fit to work, they issue the attendance certificate, not the medical certificate. In telemedicine, the rule is the same, with one additional requirement: Resolução CFM 2.314/2022 requires the doctor's name, CRM number and address, patient identification, date, time and an ICP-Brasil digital signature. At Global Health, the online clinical appointment follows this rule: the doctor issues the attendance certificate and, when they assess incapacity, the online medical certificate with a digital signature. The appointment does not guarantee time off; the doctor decides.Código de Ética Médica, capítulo X and Resolução CFM 2.314/2022, art. 13.Global Health BrasilNext stepsThe doctor assesses and documents the appointment.
