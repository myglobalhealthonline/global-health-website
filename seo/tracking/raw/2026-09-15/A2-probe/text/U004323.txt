MónicaDr. Mónica Fabiana Cornejo RománConsultant Psychiatrist | Specialist in NeuropsychopharmacologyDr. Mónica Fabiana Cornejo Román is a consultant psychiatrist with extensive experience and broad clinical practice in inpatient and outpatient mental health services in Spain and Argentina. She holds a degree in Medicine from the Universidad Nacional de Tucumán and completed her full psychiatry training at Hospital Borda, with support from Universidad Maimónides. Her medical qualification is recognized in Spain.She recently completed her specialized supplementary training at Hospital del Mar. Parc de Salut Mar (Barcelona) as part of the recognition process for her psychiatric specialty in Spain, with rotations in acute and subacute psychiatry, psychiatric emergency care, inpatient care, liaison psychiatry, complex psychopharmacology, and multidisciplinary teamwork.Dr. Cornejo has worked as a consultant psychiatrist in several leading institutions in Barcelona, including Fundación Vidal y Barraquer, Hospital Universitari Sagrat Cor, Centro de Salud Mental de Badalona (BSA) and Hospital Mare de Déu de la Mercè, providing outpatient care, liaison psychiatry, emergency psychiatry, suicide risk management, inpatient treatment, and referrals to specialized programs such as treatment-resistant depression, ADHD, and autism spectrum disorders.Previously, she practiced for more than six years in Argentina with OSDE, Hospital Santojanni, Medicus SA and Centro Larrea de Salud Mental, including high-volume emergency psychiatry shifts and complex inpatient care. Her clinical experience covers advanced psychopharmacology, mood and anxiety disorders, psychotic disorders, addictions, liaison psychiatry, suicide risk management, chronic pain, and medico-legal reports.She holds postgraduate qualifications, including a Master’s Degree in Clinical Neuropsychopharmacology, a University Specialization in Legal Medicine and a specialization in Pain Assessment and Treatment. She has participated in clinical research projects in collaboration with CIBERSAM at Hospital Sant Pau and has published in the fields of bioethics and psychiatry.Dr. Cornejo is a full member of the American Psychiatric Association and is fluent in Spanish with strong reading proficiency in English.
