It is not a GP consultation. The clinical depth, investigation review capability, and management complexity are at specialist consultant level.Can a cardiologist assess me properly without a physical examination?For the majority of cardiovascular assessment, management review, and second opinion consultations. Yes. A comprehensive clinical history, review of existing investigations, and risk stratification can all be conducted via video call to specialist standard. Where physical examination findings, heart sounds, murmurs, peripheral signs, are needed, your cardiologist will advise on in-person assessment and coordinate the appropriate pathway.Can my existing ECG or echocardiogram be reviewed during the consultation?Yes. Bring any existing investigation results, reports, or clinical letters to your consultation. Your cardiologist will review these in full and provide specialist-level interpretation, which is frequently the most valuable part of the consultation for patients who have had investigations but not had them reviewed by a cardiologist.What if I need a stress test or echocardiogram?If further investigations are clinically indicated, your cardiologist will coordinate these through our Referral & Diagnostic Investigation service. You will leave the consultation with a clear investigation pathway and the appropriate referral documentation.Can I use this service for a second opinion on a cardiac diagnosis?Yes. Second opinion consultations are a core part of this service. Whether you want an independent review of a diagnosis, a management plan, or an investigation finding, including opinions received from another cardiologist or in another country, your cardiologist will conduct a full review and provide a clear, honest, evidence-based assessment.Is this service suitable for patients with a pacemaker or ICD?Yes. For review and management consultations. Device checks and programming require in-person attendance at a device clinic. Your cardiologist will advise on the appropriate follow-up pathway for device-related concerns.Can I use this service if I have had a heart attack or cardiac procedure?Yes. For post-event management review, secondary prevention planning, and ongoing cardiovascular management. If you have recently had a cardiac event and have acute or concerning symptoms, attend your nearest emergency department rather than booking a remote consultation.What if my concern turns out to be an emergency during the consultation?If your cardiologist determines that your presentation requires emergency assessment, you will be directed to your nearest emergency department immediately. Patient safety is always the clinical priority.How do I book an online cardiology consultation in Ireland?Choose the cardiology service, select an available clinician and appointment time, then complete the booking. Before the secure video call, upload any ECG, echocardiogram, blood test results, medication list or relevant letters you want the cardiologist to review.
