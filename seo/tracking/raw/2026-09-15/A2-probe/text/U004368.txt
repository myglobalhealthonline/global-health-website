The clinical psychologist provides psychotherapy — structured psychological intervention. For many conditions, the most effective approach combines both. Your psychiatrist advises you on the most appropriate level of support and can guide you on the most suitable type of psychotherapy for your condition.What is the difference between this consultation and the mental health assessment with a primary care physician?Mental health evaluation with a general practitioner is appropriate for the initial screening of anxiety, mild to moderate depression, and stress — with referral to psychiatry where indicated. This specialist consultation is conducted by a psychiatrist and provides formal psychiatric diagnosis, evaluation of complex disorders such as bipolar disorder or ADHD, and access to the full range of psychiatric pharmacological treatment options.Can the psychiatrist prescribe psychiatric medication through this service?Yes — including antidepressants, mood stabilizers, ADHD medication, and other psychiatric pharmacological treatment options where clinically indicated. Benzodiazepines and certain controlled medications have specific requirements in Spain — your psychiatrist will inform you about the available options and any applicable limitations. No specific medication can be confirmed before the evaluation.Can the psychiatrist diagnose ADHD in adults?Yes. The formal diagnostic evaluation of ADHD in adults is a central part of this service. Diagnosis requires a complete structured assessment — it cannot be made in a single brief session. Your psychiatrist informs you about the appropriate diagnostic process for your presentation.Can the psychiatrist diagnose bipolar disorder?Yes. The differential diagnosis between unipolar depression and bipolar disorder, and between the different types of bipolar disorder, requires specialized psychiatric evaluation. It is one of the diagnoses most frequently missed or delayed in primary care — specialized psychiatric evaluation is especially valuable here.Can I obtain a psychiatric second opinion through this service?Yes. The specialized psychiatric second opinion is one of the frequent uses of this service — especially regarding diagnoses, treatment plans, or medication received from another psychiatrist.Can the psychiatrist order my psychiatric hospital admission?Voluntary admission can be coordinated with the appropriate services. Involuntary admission is regulated by Article 763 of the Civil Procedure Act and requires judicial authorization — it cannot be initiated through this service. If your situation requires urgent admission, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.What happens if I am in crisis during or after the consultation?If you are in crisis, use the resources at the top of this page — Suicide Helpline 024 or 112 in case of immediate danger. This service is not a crisis intervention service.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service.
