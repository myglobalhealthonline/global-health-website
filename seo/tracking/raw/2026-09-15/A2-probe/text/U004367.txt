The therapeutic approaches our clinical psychologists use include:Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)The approach with the strongest scientific evidence for most anxiety disorders and depression. Helps the brain process traumatic memories that have become blocked, reducing their emotional impact.Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)Particularly useful for difficulties in emotional regulation, borderline personality disorder, and self-destructive behaviours. Works on mindfulness skills, distress tolerance, emotional regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness.Brief Psychodynamic PsychotherapyUseful when current difficulties have roots in past experiences and relational patterns. The psychiatrist is a medical specialist who can formally diagnose mental disorders and prescribe psychiatric medication. For many people, working with both together is the most effective approach. If you need pharmacological evaluation or psychiatric medication management, our specialist psychiatry service is the appropriate one.How many sessions will I need?It depends on your presentation and your goals. Some problems are effectively addressed in 8–12 sessions with approaches such as CBT. Others — work on complex trauma, personality patterns, or more deeply rooted conditions — require longer-term work. Your psychologist will propose an initial plan in the first consultation and review it with you regularly.How often are the sessions?Generally once a week at the start of therapeutic work, moving to every two weeks or monthly in more advanced phases. Your psychologist adjusts the frequency to your situation and progress.Is online psychotherapy covered by Spanish private health insurance?It depends on your policy.
