Your cardiologist interprets it in full before the call and explains the findings in detail during the consultation. This is an established practice in telecardiology.I have symptoms of palpitations — is this urgent?Palpitations with dizziness, loss of consciousness, or chest pain are urgent — call 112 immediately. Palpitations without associated severe symptoms, especially if they are episodic and not worsening, are appropriate for non-urgent cardiology assessment through this service.What tests should I bring to the consultation?Everything you have relevant — ECG, echocardiogram report, Holter report, stress test report, recent lab results, letters from previous cardiologists, list of your current cardiac medications. The more information your cardiologist provides, the more complete the evaluation will be.Can the cardiologist adjust my medication?Yes — including antihypertensive, antiarrhythmic, anticoagulant, diuretic medication, and other cardiac medications where clinically indicated, at the specialist’s discretion after a complete evaluation.Can the cardiologist request an echocardiogram or Holter monitor?Yes. Where these tests are clinically indicated, your cardiologist issues the request on the same day. Private echocardiography and Holter monitoring are available in cardiology clinics and private hospitals throughout Spain — in many cities with availability within a few days.Can I get a second cardiological opinion?Yes. A specialized cardiology second opinion is one of the common uses of this service — especially regarding uncertain diagnoses, test findings that have not been well explained, or before committing to significant cardiology interventions.Is this service suitable for pre-sport cardiovascular evaluation?Yes — for adults who want to start intense physical activity or have sports-related cardiology concerns. The official sports medical examination for federation licenses requires an in-person physical examination — but the pre-sport cardiology evaluation at the specialist medicine level can be carried out with review of tests by telemedicine.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service. The clinical documentation issued is provided at the full private price regardless of insurance status.Can I use this service from anywhere in Spain?Yes — including the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands, Ceuta, and Melilla.
