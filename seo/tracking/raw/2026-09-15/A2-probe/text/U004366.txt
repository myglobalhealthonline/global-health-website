A detailed parental history of the symptoms combined with visual observation of the child during the call allows an experienced doctor to make a clinical assessment of most common childhood illnesses. Where the presentation requires an in-person physical examination — for example, to assess breathing sounds, examine the ear, or palpate the abdomen — your doctor will clearly advise you and coordinate the appropriate referral.My child has a fever — can I use this service?Yes — for children older than 3 months. Your doctor evaluates the severity, the likely cause, and the appropriate management. For infants under 3 months, fever is a medical emergency — go to the nearest emergency department immediately without booking a video consultation.Can the doctor prescribe antibiotics for my child?Yes — where clinically indicated after a complete evaluation. In Spain, all antibiotics require a medical prescription. Your doctor evaluates your child's presentation and prescribes the appropriate antibiotic where clinical evidence justifies it.Do I need to have an assigned pediatrician to use this service?No. This service is available to all families in Spain — no prior registration, no assigned pediatrician required, no health insurance necessary.My child has received a letter or diagnosis from a Spanish pediatrician that I do not understand — can you help me?Yes. Bring the letter or results to the appointment and your doctor will review and explain the findings and clinical recommendations clearly in the language you prefer.Is this service suitable for adolescents?Yes. Adolescents’ health concerns — including acne, menstrual health, mental well-being and weight — are part of this service. For adolescents aged 16 or older, the doctor assesses whether the young person can consent independently. For those under 16, a parent or guardian must be present throughout the consultation.What happens if my child needs to go to the emergency room?If your doctor determines that your child needs urgent or in-person urgent care, you will be informed immediately and clearly — including what to say when you arrive at the emergency department and which hospital is most appropriate for your location in Spain. Your doctor may issue a brief clinical summary if this would be useful.Can I obtain a school absence note through this service?Yes. School absence certificates may be issued at the clinician’s discretion after evaluation — certifying that the child was evaluated and is not fit to attend school, without specifying clinical details.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service. The clinical documentation issued through this service is provided at the full private price regardless of insurance status.Can I use this service from anywhere in Spain?Yes — including the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.
