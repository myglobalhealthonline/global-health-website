He also holds an MBA in Health Management, Innovation and Services from PUC-RS.He currently works in telemedicine integrated with primary care, with experience in a hybrid Digital Clinic model inside a Family Health Strategy primary care unit in São Paulo, one of the most innovative telemedicine formats in the Brazilian public health system (SUS), alongside experience on digital urgent care platforms for private health plans, treating patients of all ages across every region of Brazil.
