Original language of the release: Czech.Read the originalPrague, Czech Republic — September 2026The company is headquartered in the Czech Republic and operates internationally, gradually expanding its presence in Europe and beyond. The Global Health brand is registered with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO).The Czech Republic is an important market for Global Health and a significant step in the company's international development. Global Health currently operates in the Czech Republic, Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania and Brazil, and is preparing further expansion into European and non-European markets.Convenient access to online healthcareThe Global Health platform was designed to make access to healthcare simpler and more pleasant. Patients can book a medical consultation directly online and choose an available service, doctor and preferred time, without having to book by phone.In the Czech Republic, Global Health currently provides mainly online consultations with general practitioners, together with other services, including:online consultations with a general practitioner,consultations about chronic health conditions,travel medicine consultations,weight management consultations,consultations about continuing treatment, where medically appropriate,assessment of a patient's health and recommendations on next steps.Where appropriate and permitted by Czech law, the doctor may provide the patient with relevant medical recommendations and medical documentation, or issue an electronic prescription where medically indicated.Healthcare in a language the patient understandsOne of Global Health's distinctive features is the multilingualism of its international medical network.Patients can consult doctors who speak a range of languages, including English. This is especially practical for the large international community of foreigners who live and work in the Czech Republic. For foreign residents who may find it difficult to describe their health problems in Czech, being able to discuss their health in English or another language they understand well makes access to healthcare significantly easier.More than online consultationsGlobal Health is not just a platform for booking video consultations. It is designed as a digital environment in which patients can manage their healthcare.Through their personal account, patients can:book and manage their medical appointments online,view their order history,manage and view their invoices,manage their health information and the data available on the platform,access relevant medical documents,communicate directly with their doctor through offline messages, which lets patients send and receive messages without booking a new video consultation for every question or follow-up.The platform thus creates an ongoing digital connection between patient and doctor that is not limited to the video consultation itself.Simple booking from anywhereThe booking process is designed to be quick and simple. The patient visits the Global Health platform, chooses an available health service, doctor and time, and completes the booking online.The consultation then takes place remotely, so the patient can receive care from the comfort of home, from work or while travelling, where an online consultation is clinically appropriate.A Czech company with an international visionAlthough Global Health is expanding abroad, the Czech Republic remains an important part of the company's identity and development.Global Health is building an international healthcare network while keeping a strong European base. The company currently operates in six countries: the Czech Republic, Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania and Brazil.The company is now preparing the next phase of its international expansion, with the aim of entering further European markets and selected markets outside Europe.We want healthcare to be simpler, more accessible and better connected for patients. Launching our platform in the Czech Republic matters enormously to us, because Global Health is a Czech company with an international vision. The Czech Republic also has a large and diverse international community, and we believe that being able to talk to a doctor in English and other languages can make access to healthcare significantly easier.Dr Tiago Miguel Figueira, Co-Founder & CEO, Global HealthAbout Global HealthGlobal Health is a Czech healthcare company operating an international online healthcare platform that connects patients with doctors and health services. The company currently operates in the Czech Republic, Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania and Brazil, and is preparing further international expansion. Global Health provides online medical consultations and digital health services, enabling patients to book appointments, manage their health information and communicate with their doctors through a single digital platform. The Global Health brand is registered with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO).
