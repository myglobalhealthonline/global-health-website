This does not necessarily mean that any doctor was wrong — medicine has inherent uncertainties and different opinions are possible and legitimate.What documents should I bring to the second opinion consultation?Anything that is available — test reports, medical reports, current medication list, letters from other doctors, biopsy results. The more information the doctor has, the more complete the evaluation will be.Can the doctor review a specialist's diagnosis?Yes — the general practitioner can review and comment on diagnoses and treatment plans from any specialty, based on the available scientific evidence and the documentation provided.
