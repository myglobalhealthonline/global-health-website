For most people who cannot shift weight, there are underlying medical, hormonal, and metabolic factors at play that no generic diet plan addresses — because they require clinical assessment, not calorie counting.Our doctors, registered with the Czech Medical Chamber (ČLK), offer comprehensive weight management consultations by secure video call — identifying the clinical factors contributing to your weight, building a personalised management plan, and providing the medical supervision that makes sustainable change possible.This is not a diet programme. It is medical care, provided by doctors, for a medical condition.Who This Service Is ForThis consultation is appropriate for adults who:Have difficulty losing weight despite dietary and lifestyle changesHave a BMI of 25 or above and are seeking structured medical supportHave weight-related conditions — including pre-diabetes, insulin resistance, hypertension, or PCOS — that are contributing to or complicating their weightWant to understand whether they may be candidates for medical weight management therapies, including newer metabolic therapies, following clinical assessmentHave tried diet and exercise approaches without sustainable resultsAre preparing for or considering bariatric surgery and are seeking medical assessment and optimisationWant ongoing medical monitoring of their metabolic health alongside weight managementWhat Your Consultation IncludesComprehensive medical assessmentYour doctor reviews your medical history, current medications, metabolic risk factors, hormonal profile, dietary patterns, physical activity, sleep quality, stress levels, and previous weight management attempts — building a complete clinical picture of the factors contributing to your weight.Identification of underlying causesWeight gain is rarely a simple equation. Your doctor assesses hormonal contributors — including thyroid dysfunction, insulin resistance, PCOS, and cortisol dysregulation — metabolic factors, medication-related weight gain, and lifestyle elements that interact with your biology.Personalised weight management planBased on the assessment, your doctor develops a structured, evidence-based plan adapted to your BMI, health status, lifestyle, and long-term goals — including nutritional strategy, behavioural approach, metabolic health optimisation, and clinical recommendations at the doctor's professional discretion.Assessment for medical weight management therapiesFor patients who meet the clinical criteria, your doctor assesses eligibility for evidence-based medical weight management therapies as part of a comprehensive clinical assessment. Eligibility is determined by clinical criteria — BMI, weight-related comorbidities, previous treatment history, contraindications, and general health status — not by patient preference.Investigation requests where indicatedWhere blood tests are clinically indicated — thyroid function, fasting glucose, HbA1c, hormonal profile, lipid panel — your doctor issues the request on the same day.Ongoing reviewWeight management is a long-term clinical process. Follow-up consultations are available to monitor progress, review treatment response, and adjust your plan.Conditions Commonly AssessedMetabolicOverweight and obesity — BMI 25 and aboveInsulin resistance and pre-diabetesType 2 diabetes — weight management as part of metabolic controlMetabolic syndromeHigh cholesterol and triglyceridesHormonalPolycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)Hypothyroidism associated with weight gainCortisol dysregulation and stress-related weight gainMetabolic changes associated with menopauseCardiovascularHypertension associated with excess weightCardiovascular risk reduction through weight managementOtherNon-alcoholic fatty liver diseaseObstructive sleep apnoeaJoint pain related to excess weightFertility concerns related to weight and hormonal statusWhy Medical Supervision Makes a DifferenceMost weight management approaches fail not through lack of effort but because they do not address the underlying clinical picture. Hormonal imbalance, insulin resistance, medication side effects, sleep disorders, and metabolic dysfunction all contribute to weight gain in ways that diet and exercise alone cannot resolve.Medical weight management directly addresses these factors — providing clinical supervision, evidence-based treatment, and ongoing monitoring that makes sustainable change possible for patients who have not succeeded with other approaches.Weight management consultations are conducted by doctors registered with the Czech Medical Chamber (ČLK), in accordance with Czech telemedicine regulations under Act No. 372/2011 Coll. and Decree No. 30/2025 Coll.
