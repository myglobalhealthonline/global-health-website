Dermatology is one of the medical specialties best suited to visual assessment, and a video consultation allows your GP to observe the condition directly. Where a condition cannot be adequately assessed at general practice level, your GP refers you to our specialist dermatology service within the same platform.What is the difference between this consultation and the specialist dermatology consultation?This consultation is carried out by registered primary care physicians and covers most acute and non-urgent skin conditions. The specialist dermatology consultation is carried out by registered dermatologists from Global Health and is appropriate for complex conditions, treatment-resistant conditions, or those requiring high-resolution dermoscopy, consideration of isotretinoin, or coordinated biopsy. If you are unsure which level is appropriate, start here — your primary care physician will refer you if necessary.Can I get treatment for acne through this service?Yes — for mild to moderate acne. Your GP carries out a full assessment and advises on the most appropriate management approach. If your presentation justifies consideration of isotretinoin, your GP refers you directly to our specialist dermatology service within the same platform.I have a mole that has changed — how urgent is it?Moles that have changed warrant prompt clinical evaluation. Book an appointment and show your primary care doctor the lesion during the video consultation. Your doctor evaluates using the standard ABCDE criteria and, where indicated, refers you to specialist dermatology for assessment with dermoscopy.Can I use this service for my child's eczema?Yes. Eczema in infants and children is one of the most common pediatric skin presentations and is a central part of this service.Can I get a referral to dermatology through this service?Yes — both an internal referral to our specialist dermatology service at Global Health, and an external referral letter to a dermatologist of your choice where clinically indicated.My rash is spreading rapidly and I feel unwell — what should I do?A rash that spreads quickly accompanied by fever, difficulty breathing, facial swelling, or severe general malaise is a medical emergency. Call 112 or go to your nearest emergency department immediately — do not book a video consultation.Can I use this service for a sun reaction?Yes. Sunburn and sun-related skin reactions are especially common in tourists and visitors in Spain. Your primary care doctor assesses the severity and advises on appropriate management.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service. Clinical documentation issued through this service is dispensed at the full private price regardless of insurance status.Can I use this service from any point in Spain?Yes — including the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.
