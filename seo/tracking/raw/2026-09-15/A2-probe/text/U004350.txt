A detailed medical history is the most powerful diagnostic tool in the evaluation of hair loss, and a complete history can be taken by video call. Your doctor also asks you to show the affected areas during the call. Where physical examination findings or scalp biopsy are needed, your doctor advises you on appropriate referral.Do I need blood tests before my appointment?No. Your doctor evaluates during the consultation whether the tests are indicated and requests the lab work the same day if so. Bring any recent results you have — your doctor reviews them in full.Can hair loss be reversed?It depends on the type and how long it has been progressing. Androgenic alopecia responds better to treatment started early, when the follicles are thinning rather than already lost. Telogen effluvium is generally self-limited and resolves once the underlying cause is treated. Hair loss related to nutritional deficiency responds well to correcting the deficiency. Scarring alopecia requires urgent specialist evaluation because it can cause permanent loss if not treated.Is this service suitable for women with hair loss?Yes. Female hair loss is a central part of this service. The clinical picture in women is often more complex than male androgenetic alopecia— involving hormonal, nutritional, and autoimmune factors — and benefits significantly from a structured clinical evaluation.What if my hair loss is related to a medication I am taking?Hair loss related to medications is evaluated as part of this consultation. Your doctor reviews your current medication, identifies whether any may be contributing to your hair loss, and advises on appropriate management.Can I get a referral to dermatology through this service?Yes.
