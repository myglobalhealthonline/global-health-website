The doctor assesses the severity of the symptoms, rules out organic causes, provides guidance on initial management, and refers to a psychiatrist or psychologist when indicated. This avoids an unnecessary referral and helps to better understand what is happening before seeking a specialist.Can a general practitioner prescribe antidepressants?Yes — when clinically indicated after a complete evaluation, at the physician’s sole discretion. Commonly used antidepressants such as fluoxetine, sertraline, and escitalopram may be prescribed by general practitioners. For more complex cases or those requiring specific psychiatric medications, the physician refers the patient to a psychiatrist.Does this service offer psychotherapy?No. This is a medical evaluation service with a general practitioner — not psychotherapy. For structured psychological follow-up, a clinical psychologist is the most appropriate professional. The doctor can guide you on how to seek this follow-up.And if I have a crisis during or after the appointment?If you are in crisis, use the resources at the top of this page — CVV (188) or SAMU (192) in case of immediate danger. This service is not a crisis intervention service.Will the doctor judge me for what I tell them?No. The consultation is a confidential, nonjudgmental space. The doctor is there to help you understand what is happening and find the best path for your case.Is the consultation confidential?Yes. Everything you share during the consultation is protected by medical confidentiality and by the LGPD (Law 13.709/2018). The only situations in which confidentiality may be weighed are when there is a serious and immediate risk to your life or to the safety of other people — the doctor discusses this with you at the start of the consultation when relevant.Can I use this service if I am already seeing a psychologist or psychiatrist?Yes. If you already have specialized follow-up and need a complementary general clinical evaluation — to rule out organic causes, review non-psychiatric medication, or obtain a medical certificate — this appointment is appropriate for your case.Can I use this service from any state in Brazil?Yes.
