The physician evaluates contraindications before prescribing any hormonal method. No prescription can be guaranteed before the consultation.Can I treat a urinary tract infection via teleconsultation?Yes — for uncomplicated urinary tract infections with a typical clinical presentation. The doctor assesses the symptoms and issues an electronic prescription when clinically indicated. If you have recurrent urinary tract infections, the doctor also provides guidance on evaluation and prevention.Can I treat candidiasis via teleconsultation?Yes — for candidiasis with a typical clinical presentation in women without risk factors suggesting a complicated case. The doctor evaluates and prescribes treatment when indicated.Can the doctor request a transvaginal ultrasound?The doctor may request a transvaginal ultrasound to investigate gynecological complaints — but the examination must be performed in person at an imaging clinic. The doctor issues the request during the consultation.Can I use this service during pregnancy?Yes — for general health complaints during pregnancy that do not require a physical examination or in-person obstetric monitoring. Regular prenatal care requires in-person visits with an obstetrician. If you have concerning symptoms during pregnancy, the doctor will clearly tell you whether you need urgent in-person evaluation.Can the doctor help with menopause symptoms?Yes. The general practitioner evaluates the symptoms, orders hormonal tests when indicated, and advises on management options. When hormone therapy or another specialized intervention is indicated, the doctor refers the patient to a gynecologist specializing in climacteric.Can I use this service from any state in Brazil?Yes. Under Brazilian federal law (Law 14.510/2022), acts performed via telemedicine are valid throughout Brazil.
