Assessment and prescription of malaria prophylaxis is one of the main reasons to use this service. The doctor will assess the most suitable antimalarial for your destination, itinerary, and health profile — and issue an eRecept directly. You redeem the code at any pharmacy in the Czech Republic.Can I get a yellow fever certificate through this service?No. Yellow fever vaccination and the International Certificate of Vaccination must be administered and issued in person at a KHS-authorised centre. The doctor will advise which destinations require certification and direct you to the appropriate centre. The UZIS locator at ockoreport.uzis.cz can help you find the nearest authorised centre.Can vaccines be given during the consultation?No. This is a clinical assessment and planning service. Vaccine administration requires an in-person visit. The doctor will advise which vaccinations are recommended and where to have them — so you arrive at the vaccination centre with a clear plan.What do I need to bring to the consultation?Your travel itinerary, destination details, planned activities, departure date, and any existing vaccination records. If you have a vaccination card from previous trips, bring it — the doctor will review it in full.How far in advance should I book?Ideally four to six weeks before departure. For last-minute travel, book as soon as possible.Can I use this service if I don't feel well after travelling?Yes. Post-travel assessment is available. If you return from a higher-risk destination with symptoms — especially fever — book an appointment as soon as possible. Certain tropical illnesses require early diagnosis.Is this service suitable for families with children?Yes. Travel health planning for families with children is a core part of this service.Is there a reason for someone with diabetes to use this service?Yes, especially. Managing diabetes while travelling — insulin storage, adjusting dosing across time zones, dietary considerations, and when to seek care abroad — requires specific medical advice that you won't get at a vaccination centre.Can the doctor issue a letter for medication I'm carrying?Yes.
