From day 15, ČSSZ pays sickness benefit for every calendar day.A Czech sick-pay calculation in 2026 has two separate stages. For an employee covered by sickness insurance, the employer pays wage compensation during the first 14 calendar days, but only for missed scheduled shifts and paid public holidays. From day 15, ČSSZ pays sickness benefit for every calendar day. Self-employed people, some agreements and cross-border cases follow different rules. Both stages use reduction thresholds, so neither amount is a simple percentage of monthly gross salary.Days 1-14: wage compensation from the employerFrom day 15: sickness benefit from ČSSZ2026 daily thresholds: CZK 1,633, 2,449 and 4,897Open the official 2026 calculatorHow Czech eNeschopenka worksQuick answerThe two stages use different calculations.From day 15, ČSSZ uses a daily assessment base.GHGlobal Health Medical TeamReviewed by the Global Health Medical Team.Wage compensationWhat the employer pays during the first 14 daysThe employer pays 60% of reduced average hourly earnings for missed shifts and paid public holidays.Payroll starts with average hourly earnings rather than the current monthly salary. For 2026, the hourly reduction thresholds are 285,78 Kč / 428,58 Kč / 856,98 Kč. Payroll counts 90% up to the first threshold, 60% between the first and second, and 30% between the second and third. Earnings above the third threshold do not enter the calculation.The employer takes 60% of the reduced result and multiplies it by the missed working hours. Two people with the same monthly salary can therefore receive different compensation when their shift patterns differ.The legal basis is Section 192 of the Czech Labour Code.ČSSZ benefitHow ČSSZ calculates sickness benefit from day 15ČSSZ uses a reduced daily assessment base and pays for every calendar day.The base normally uses assessable income from the previous 12 calendar months. The 2026 daily thresholds are 1 633 Kč / 2 449 Kč / 4 897 Kč. ČSSZ counts 90% up to the first threshold, 60% to the second, and 30% to the third.Length of absenceWhat percentage ČSSZ paysDays 15-30: 60% of the reduced daily base.Days 31-60: 66%.From day 61: 72%.Thresholds and rates were checked on 25 August 2026 with MPSV and ČSSZ.Worked methodA worked Czech sick-pay exampleA simple example shows why the two stages cannot be combined.Employer example: CZK 250 an hour is reduced to CZK 225. Sixty percent is CZK 135 per missed hour. Nine eight-hour shifts give an estimate of CZK 9,720 before rounding.ČSSZ example: a daily base of CZK 1,500 is reduced to CZK 1,350.
