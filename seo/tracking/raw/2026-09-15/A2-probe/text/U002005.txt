For the vast majority of presentations. Most hair loss conditions are assessed and managed at GP level, including male and female pattern hair loss, telogen effluvium, nutritional deficiency-related hair loss, and mild alopecia areata. Complex or scarring conditions are referred to dermatology. Your GP is the correct first point of contact for hair loss assessment.Do I need blood tests before the consultation?No. Your doctor will assess during the consultation whether blood tests are indicated and, if so, issue the request on the same day. Bring any existing blood test results if you have had them done recently. Your doctor will review them in full.Can hair loss be reversed?It depends on the type and how long it has been progressing. Androgenetic alopecia, male and female pattern hair loss, responds best to treatment initiated early, when follicles are thinning rather than lost. Telogen effluvium is typically self-limiting and resolves once the underlying trigger is addressed. Nutritional deficiency-related hair loss responds well to correction of the deficiency. Scarring alopecia requires urgent specialist assessment as it can cause permanent loss if untreated.Can women use this service for hair loss?Yes. Female hair loss is a core part of this service. The clinical picture for women is frequently more complex than male pattern baldness, involving hormonal, nutritional, and autoimmune factors, and benefits significantly from structured clinical assessment.What if my hair loss is related to a medication I am taking?Medication-related hair loss is assessed as part of this consultation. Your doctor will review your current medications, identify whether any are contributing to your hair loss, and advise on appropriate management, including whether medication adjustment is clinically appropriate in the context of your overall health.Can I use this service if I have already tried over-the-counter treatments?Yes. Many patients have already tried topical minoxidil or other over-the-counter options before seeking a clinical assessment. Bring details of what you have tried and your assessment of the response. This is clinically useful information that your doctor will incorporate into the management plan.At what age is this service appropriate?Hair loss can begin in the twenties for both men and women. This service is appropriate for any adult experiencing hair loss or thinning regardless of age. Adolescent hair loss should be assessed through our Paediatric GP Consultation in the first instance.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
