Many employers accept it for short absences, but this depends on the collective agreement and company policy. It is not a parte de baja: temporary incapacity benefit requires the parte issued by the public health service or, for work-related conditions, the mutua.Can I use this service if I am self-employed?Yes. The private medical certificate documents your health condition. If you contribute for temporary incapacity, the parte de baja is issued by your public health service doctor (or the mutua for work-related conditions) and the benefit is usually managed by your mutua colaboradora; the doctor can guide you.Does the certificate specify the diagnosis?The certificate documents the clinical assessment and the doctor's opinion on your fitness for work that day; it is not a parte de baja. If you prefer the specific diagnosis not to appear, for privacy, tell the doctor at the start of the consultation.Can I obtain a retroactive certificate?Not routinely. Our doctors cannot clinically certify a period of illness that they did not evaluate at the time. If you have been sick for days, mention it at the start of the consultation.Does my company have to accept the private medical certificate?The legal obligation depends on the collective bargaining agreement and the internal policy of each company. In general, most employers accept private medical certificates for short-term absences.Can I make a reservation if I have been sick for several days?Yes — but mention from the start of the consultation how many days you have had symptoms. The doctor will assess your current clinical situation and advise you on the most appropriate documentation for your case.Can I use this service from anywhere in Spain?Yes — including the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.
