Seeking a second opinion is a clinically encouraged and ethically supported practice. The Irish Medical Council explicitly supports patients' right to seek an independent clinical perspective. Your current doctor should not take offence, and if they do, that itself is clinically significant.Will the second opinion definitely be different from the first?Not necessarily, and an honest second opinion that confirms the original diagnosis is just as valuable as one that differs. Confirmation with understanding is clinically meaningful. What you are seeking is an independent perspective from a doctor who has reviewed the same evidence without prior assumptions. Whatever that perspective turns out to be.Can your doctors review specialist consultant reports?Yes. At GP level. Our doctors can review specialist letters, consultant reports, and clinical documentation and provide a GP-level perspective on the clinical reasoning, diagnosis, and management plan. Where a genuine specialist-level second opinion is warranted, for complex surgical decisions, oncology management, or other specialist areas, your doctor will advise and coordinate appropriate specialist referral.Can I use this service if my clinical documents are in another language?Yes. Our doctors can review documentation in Portuguese, Spanish, Czech, and Romanian alongside English. If your documents are in a language other than these, bring them anyway and your doctor will work with what is available alongside your account of what you have been told.What if the second opinion raises concerns about my original diagnosis?If your doctor's independent assessment raises questions about your original diagnosis or management plan, they will explain clearly what the concerns are, what the evidence supports, and what appropriate next steps look like, including whether further investigation or specialist review is warranted. Your doctor will not alarm you without clinical basis, but they will be honest with you about what the clinical picture suggests.Can I get a second opinion on a mental health diagnosis?Yes. At GP level. Our doctors can provide an independent GP-level perspective on a mental health diagnosis, management plan, or medication approach. For specialist psychiatric second opinions, your doctor will advise on the appropriate referral pathway.Can I use this service to get a second opinion on a diagnosis received abroad?Yes. This is one of the most common uses of this service. Bring any relevant clinical documentation from your home country and your doctor will review it in full, providing an independent perspective within the Irish and EU clinical context.How is this different from a standard Medical Consultation?A standard Acute Medical Consultation is for assessing new or current symptoms. A Second Opinion Consultation is specifically structured around reviewing an existing diagnosis, clinical letter, or management plan, with dedicated time for document review, clinical reasoning discussion, and evidence-based assessment of what you have been told. It is a different clinical task that requires a different structure.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
