The medical history is the most powerful diagnostic tool in musculoskeletal medicine, and a complete history can be taken by video call. Your doctor also observes your movement, posture, and functional ability during the call. Where physical examination findings are needed that cannot be assessed remotely, your doctor advises you on appropriate in-person evaluation.What happens if I need an MRI or X-ray?If imaging tests are clinically indicated based on the evaluation, your doctor issues an imaging request the same day. You can book the imaging at a private radiology center of your choice in Spain — private radiology is widely available in major cities and in many regional centers.Can I get a referral to physiotherapy through this service?Yes. Referral letters to physiotherapy are issued when they are clinically indicated — addressed to the physiotherapist or clinic of your choice. Your doctor can advise you on what to look for in a physiotherapist for your specific condition.What is cauda equina syndrome and why is it mentioned?Cauda equina syndrome is a rare but serious spinal emergency that presents as back pain associated with a change in bladder or bowel function. If you have back pain with any change in bladder or bowel function, go to the nearest emergency department immediately.Is this service suitable for sports injuries?Yes. Sports and exercise-related musculoskeletal injuries are a central part of this service — including acute injuries, overuse conditions, and return-to-sport assessment after injury.I already have imaging test results — can the doctor review them?Yes. Your doctor reviews them in full and incorporates the findings into the management plan.Do I need to have had imaging tests before booking?No. Your doctor evaluates whether imaging tests are clinically indicated and orders them where appropriate.
