The evaluation of antimalarial prophylaxis is one of the main reasons for using this service. Your primary care doctor evaluates the most appropriate antimalarial for your destination, itinerary, and health profile — and issues the clinical documentation directly. You present it at any pharmacy of your choice in Spain.Can I obtain the yellow fever certificate through this service?No. Yellow fever vaccination and the International Certificate of Vaccination must be administered and issued in person at an authorized vaccination center. Your doctor advises you on which destinations require this certification and directs you to the appropriate authorized center in Spain after your consultation.Can vaccines be administered during the consultation?No. This is a clinical evaluation and planning service. Vaccine administration requires an in-person visit to a vaccination center. Your doctor advises you on which vaccines you need and where to go — so that you arrive at the vaccination center with a clear plan.What do I need to bring to my appointment?Your travel itinerary, destination details, planned activities, departure date, and any existing vaccination records. If you have a vaccination card from previous trips, bring it — your doctor will review it in full.How far in advance should I book?Ideally four to six weeks before departure. For last-minute trips, book as soon as possible — your doctor will advise you on what is feasible.Can I use this service if I have become ill after traveling?Yes. Post-travel evaluation is available. If you return from a higher-risk destination with symptoms — especially fever — book promptly. Certain tropical diseases require early diagnosis and treatment.Is this service suitable for families with children?Yes. Travel health planning for families with children is a central part of this service.Can the doctor issue a letter for the medication I am taking?Yes. Your doctor issues a travel health summary letter documenting your medication, conditions, and medical devices — useful for customs, border health authorities, and airline requirements.I have diabetes — is this service relevant to me?Yes, especially. Managing diabetes during travel — insulin storage, time zone adjustment for dosing, dietary considerations, and when to seek care abroad — requires specific medical advice that a vaccination center cannot provide.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service. The clinical documentation issued through this service is provided at the full private price regardless of insurance status.Also available inSpanishPortugueseCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
