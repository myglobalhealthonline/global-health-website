A detailed parental history of symptoms combined with visual observation of the child during the call allows an experienced doctor to clinically assess most common childhood illnesses. Where the presentation requires an in-person examination — for example, assessing breath sounds, an ear examination, or abdominal palpation — the doctor will advise clearly and coordinate an appropriate referral.My child has a fever — can I use this service?Yes — for children over 3 months old with a fever. The doctor will assess severity, likely cause, and appropriate management. For infants under 3 months, fever is a medical emergency — go to the nearest emergency department immediately without booking a video consultation.Do we need to have a registered paediatrician to use this service?No. This service is available to all families in the Czech Republic, including families without a registered children's doctor. It also works alongside your existing paediatrician for situations where you need advice outside office hours.What if my child needs to go to the emergency department?If the doctor determines that your child needs urgent or emergency in-person care, you will be informed immediately and clearly — including what to say when you arrive at the emergency department. The doctor can issue a brief clinical summary letter if that would be helpful.Can I get a school absence note through this service?Yes. School absence notes and similar documentation can be issued at the doctor's clinical discretion after assessment — confirming that the child was assessed and is unable to attend school, without specifying clinical details.Is this service suitable for teenagers?Yes. Adolescent health concerns — including acne, menstrual health, mental wellbeing, and weight — are part of this service. For adolescents aged 16 and over, the doctor will assess whether the young person can consent independently. For those under 16, a parent or legal guardian must be present.Also available inCzechPortugueseSpanishRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
