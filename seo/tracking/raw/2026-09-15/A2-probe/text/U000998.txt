Clinical history is the strongest diagnostic tool in musculoskeletal medicine, and a full history can be taken over video call. The doctor will also observe your movement, posture, and functional capacity during the call. Where physical examination findings are needed that can't be assessed remotely, the doctor will advise an appropriate in-person assessment.What if I need an MRI or X-ray?If imaging is clinically indicated based on the assessment, the doctor will issue a formal imaging request the same day. You can then book imaging at a private radiology centre of your choice. Results can be reviewed at a follow-up consultation.Can I get a physiotherapy referral through this service?Yes. Physiotherapy referral letters are issued where clinically indicated — addressed to a physiotherapist or clinic of your choice.What is cauda equina syndrome and why is it mentioned?Cauda equina syndrome is a rare but serious spinal emergency caused by compression of the nerve roots at the base of the spine. It presents as lower back pain accompanied by a change in bladder or bowel function and requires urgent surgical assessment. It is mentioned because it can be mistaken for ordinary back pain. If you have lower back pain accompanied by any change in bladder or bowel function, go to the nearest emergency department immediately — do not wait for a video consultation.Is this service suitable for sports injuries?Yes. Sports and exercise-related musculoskeletal injuries are a core part of this service — including acute injuries, overuse conditions, and return-to-sport assessment after injury.I already have imaging results — can the doctor review them?Yes. Bring any existing imaging results, reports, or clinical letters to the consultation. The doctor will review them in full and incorporate them into the management plan.Do I need to have had imaging done before booking?No. The doctor will assess whether imaging is clinically indicated and request it where appropriate. If you already have imaging, bring the results — the doctor will review them in full.Also available inCzechPortugueseSpanishRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
