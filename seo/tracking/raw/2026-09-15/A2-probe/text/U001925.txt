We're building a clinic that removes barriers between doctors and the patients who need them — wherever they are.Why Online Dermatology Is Needed in Ireland Right NowAs of February 2026, more than 60,000 people are on the public waiting list for a dermatology outpatient appointment in Ireland — nearly 10% of the entire national outpatient waiting list — with around 3,600 of those cases being children. In some regions, routine wait times stretch to as long as three years. The shortage isn't a lack of qualified specialists: 114 consultant dermatologists are registered in Ireland, but only 64 currently work within the public system, leaving a significant gap between demand and public capacity.Dermatology is, by its visual nature, one of the specialties best suited to remote consultation — and teledermatology is already recognised by experts as a way to meaningfully improve efficiency and access while the underlying capacity gap is addressed.Online dermatology is one real way to help close this gap — and you can be part of it.The Position: Dermatologist for Online ConsultationsWe're looking for licensed dermatologists based in Ireland to join our online clinic. Through our platform, you'll provide remote dermatology consultations to patients across the country, with full flexibility to shape your work schedule.
100% remote — consult from anywhere, via video or image submission for review
Flexible schedule — choose the hours and days that suit you
Main or supplementary role — ideal alongside an existing practice or hospital position
E-prescribing fully integrated — no extra paperwork
What You'll Be Part Of
A genuinely international team of doctors — collaborate and connect with colleagues across Europe and Brazil
A growing platform — you're joining Global Health at a growth stage in Ireland, not a position within an already-established system
Real impact — help patients who might otherwise wait years for a dermatology appointment, from the comfort of anywhere
Ongoing support — technical, administrative, and clinical support so you can focus on medicine, not paperwork
Who We're Looking For
Valid medical registration in Ireland (Irish Medical Council)
Specialisation in Dermatology
Experience with telemedicine tools is a plus (training and onboarding support provided)
Fluent English; Portuguese, Spanish, Romanian, or Czech are a bonus given our presence in those markets
