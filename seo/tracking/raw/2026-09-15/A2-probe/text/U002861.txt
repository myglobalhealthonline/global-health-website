This page estimates how likely a bacterial cause is from your symptoms, which is what a clinician uses to decide whether that swab is worth taking. It cannot replace the swab.What is the difference between tonsillitis and strep throat?Tonsillitis describes inflamed tonsils, whatever the cause — most often a virus. Strep throat is tonsillitis or pharyngitis caused by one particular bacterium, Streptococcus pyogenes, known as group A strep. Every case of strep throat is tonsillitis; most tonsillitis is not strep throat.Do I need antibiotics for a sore throat?That is a decision for a doctor, not for this page. Most sore throats are viral, where antibiotics do nothing and still carry side effects. A high score means the odds of a bacterial cause are high enough that a doctor would usually examine you and consider testing — it does not mean antibiotics are indicated.How long should a sore throat last?Most settle within about a week, whether viral or bacterial. A sore throat that is still worsening after a week, or that comes with a fever you cannot bring down, is worth having looked at regardless of what this checker says.Why does the score subtract a point if I am over 45?Because strep throat becomes uncommon with age. The original Centor score was built for adults and over-called bacterial infection in older patients; McIsaac's age adjustment corrects for that in both directions, adding a point for children aged 3 to 14, where strep is most common.Does this checker store my answers?No. The scoring runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you select is sent to our servers or saved.Do I need a prescription for antibiotics in Portugal?Yes. Antibiotics are prescription-only in Portugal, so a pharmacy cannot dispense them for a sore throat without one. A pharmacist can advise on pain relief and tell you whether they think you should be seen, but the prescribing decision belongs to a doctor who has examined you.Where can I get a sore throat examined in Portugal?A family doctor can look at your throat and take a swab if the pattern warrants it.
