resultsWhat Your Consultation IncludesComplete Cardiovascular Assessment. Your cardiologist carries out a detailed clinical review — covering your cardiovascular symptoms, medical history, family history, risk factors, current medication, lifestyle factors, and any existing test results. This structured assessment is the foundation of all clinical recommendations.Review of Existing Tests. If you have ECGs, echocardiogram reports, Holter results, stress-test reports, or existing blood test results, your cardiologist reviews them in full during the consultation — providing specialist interpretation and clinical context.Risk Stratification. Your cardiologist assesses your overall cardiovascular risk profile using validated risk tools and advises on the prevention and management strategy appropriate to your individual risk level.Test Coordination. When further tests are clinically indicated — ECG, echocardiogram, Holter monitor, stress test, cardiac MRI, or blood tests — your cardiologist coordinates these through our Referral and Complementary Tests service, ensuring efficient access to the right tests.Specialist Management Plan. Based on the assessment, your cardiologist provides a complete management plan — including medication review and recommendations at the specialist's professional discretion, lifestyle guidance, secondary prevention strategies, and a clear follow-up pathway.Second Opinion. It is not a GP consultation. The clinical depth, ability to review tests, and management complexity are at specialist level.Can a cardiologist assess me properly without a physical examination?For most cardiovascular assessment, management review, and second-opinion consultations — yes. A complete clinical history, review of existing tests, and risk stratification can be carried out at specialist level by video call. When physical examination findings are needed — heart sounds, murmurs, peripheral signs — your cardiologist advises on an in-person assessment and coordinates the appropriate pathway.Can my existing ECG or echocardiogram be reviewed during the consultation?Yes. Bring any existing test results, reports, or clinical letters to your consultation. Your cardiologist reviews them in full and provides specialist interpretation — often the most valuable part of the consultation for patients who have already had tests done but never had them reviewed by a cardiologist.What if I need a stress test or echocardiogram?If further tests are clinically indicated, your cardiologist coordinates them through our Referral and Complementary Tests service. You leave the consultation with a clear testing pathway and the appropriate referral documentation.Can I use this service for a second opinion on a heart diagnosis?Yes. Second-opinion consultations are a core part of this service. Whether you want an independent review of a diagnosis, management plan, or test result — including opinions received from another cardiologist or in another country — your cardiologist carries out a complete review and provides a clear, honest, evidence-based assessment.Is this service suitable for patients with a pacemaker or ICD?Yes — for review and management consultations. Device check and programming requires attendance at a device clinic. Your cardiologist advises on the appropriate follow-up pathway for device-related concerns.Can I use this service if I have had a heart attack or cardiac procedure?Yes — for post-event management review, secondary prevention planning, and ongoing cardiovascular management. If you have had a recent cardiac event and have acute or concerning symptoms, go to the nearest emergency department instead of booking a remote consultation.What if my situation turns out to be an emergency during the consultation?If your cardiologist determines that your presentation requires emergency assessment, you are directed immediately to the nearest emergency department.
