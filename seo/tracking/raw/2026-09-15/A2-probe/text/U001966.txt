Expats and international residents anywhere in Ireland can see an Irish-registered doctor online — no local GP registration, no waiting list, and no need to explain yourself in a second language if you would rather not.Languages our Irish doctors consult inEach link opens the roster filtered to doctors who consult in that language:Arabic — general practice, plus cardiology and neurology. Medication you were on abroad may have a different name, a different licensed dose, or no Irish equivalent — the doctor will tell you which applies and, where needed, arrange the nearest suitable option.
