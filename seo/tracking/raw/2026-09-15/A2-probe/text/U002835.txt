Approaches used include:Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT). The most extensively researched psychological therapy, with strong evidence for anxiety disorders, depression, ADHD, health anxiety, and many other presentations. CBT focuses on the relationship between thoughts, feelings, and behaviours — identifying unhelpful patterns and developing more adaptive responses.Trauma-Focused Therapy. Including trauma-focused CBT and EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) — evidence-based approaches for PTSD, complex trauma, and trauma-related presentations.Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). A third-wave cognitive behavioural approach, focused on psychological flexibility — learning to relate differently to difficult thoughts and feelings, rather than fighting them, and clarifying personal values as a guide to meaningful action.Mindfulness-Based Approaches. Including Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT) — particularly effective for recurrent depression and anxiety — and mindfulness-based stress reduction integrated into other therapeutic modalities.Behavioural Activation. A structured, evidence-based approach for depression, focused on increasing engagement in meaningful activities and breaking the cycle of avoidance and low mood.Interpersonal Therapy (IPT). Focused on the relationship between interpersonal difficulties and psychological symptoms — particularly effective for depression in the context of relationship difficulties, grief, or life transitions.Schema Therapy. For complex, long-standing psychological difficulties rooted in early life experiences — addressing deeply held beliefs about the self, others, and the world that maintain current difficulties.Your psychologist discusses the therapeutic approach most suited to your specific presentation during the initial assessment. Psychologists hold an integrated degree and master's in Psychology, conduct formal psychological assessment, and provide evidence-based therapy. Our psychologists are registered with the OPP.How many sessions will I need?It depends on your presentation and therapeutic goals. Brief evidence-based interventions — such as CBT for specific anxiety disorders — can achieve significant improvement in 8 to 16 sessions. More complex or long-standing difficulties may require longer-term work. Your psychologist discusses the expected duration of treatment during the initial assessment and reviews this as therapy progresses.Is online psychological therapy as effective as in-person therapy?Yes — for most presentations. A substantial body of research shows that online CBT and other evidence-based therapies produce outcomes equivalent to in-person therapy for anxiety, depression, PTSD, and many other conditions. The therapeutic relationship — consistently identified as the strongest predictor of outcomes — is fully established in the online format.Can I get a formal ADHD assessment through this service?Yes. Formal psychological assessment for adult ADHD is available through our psychology service. It involves multiple assessment sessions, validated rating scales, clinical interview, and a formal written report with diagnostic conclusions and recommendations. Discuss this specifically when booking, so the appropriate structure can be allocated.Does my psychologist communicate with my doctor?With your consent, your psychologist can share a clinical summary with your doctor — supporting integrated care and ensuring your doctor has the relevant clinical context. This is particularly important where your psychological difficulties have a physical health dimension, or where medication review is relevant.Can I use this service if I am already being seen by a psychiatrist?Yes. Psychological therapy and psychiatric management often work best in combination — addressing both the psychological and biological dimensions of mental health. Your psychologist communicates with your psychiatrist when clinically relevant and with your consent.Is this service suitable for teenagers?Psychology consultations for patients under 16 require parental or guardian consent and presence. Psychological therapy in teenagers requires specific clinical considerations — your psychologist advises on suitability and approach during the initial assessment.What if my difficulties require more intensive support than outpatient psychology?If your presentation requires more intensive support — including day hospital, inpatient care, or specialist services — your psychologist advises you clearly and coordinates the appropriate referral.
