At 55% or 60%, certain family or low-income conditions may add five percentage points.Rates and increases checked on 25 August 2026 against the Segurança Social portal and Portugal's consolidated sickness-benefit legislation.The formulaHow to estimate Portuguese sickness benefitIn the usual case, daily reference earnings are calculated as R ÷ 180.R is the remuneration registered in the first six of the previous eight months. Holiday and Christmas allowances are excluded, and net salary does not replace the official record.A calculator does not decide your paymentIt explains the formula. Segurança Social calculates the benefit from your record.EstimateA worked example of Portugal sick payWith €7,200 of eligible registered earnings, the daily reference amount is €40. At 55%, the indicative daily benefit is €22.For an employee certified as unfit for work for 20 days, with a three-day waiting period, the simple calculation is 17 × €22 = €374. Exceptions, earlier periods of sick leave and your contribution status can change the official result.When it startsWhen Portuguese sick pay startsEmployees are usually paid from the fourth day. For self-employed workers, including people working with recibos verdes, payment starts on the eleventh day after a ten-day waiting period.Hospital admission, outpatient surgery and tuberculosis are among the situations with no waiting period.An autodeclaração may justify a short absence but does not create a benefit entitlement.
