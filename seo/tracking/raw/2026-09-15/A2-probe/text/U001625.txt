The Paediatric Specialist Consultation is delivered by a consultant paediatrician registered on the Specialist Division in Paediatrics with the IMC. Providing specialist-level assessment, diagnostic formulation, and management for complex, chronic, or poorly understood paediatric presentations. The specialist consultation is the appropriate choice when GP-level care has not adequately addressed your child's concern or when specialist diagnostic expertise is needed.Can a consultant paediatrician assess my child properly via video call?For the majority of specialist paediatric assessment, developmental assessment, management review, second opinion, investigation coordination, and clinical formulation, yes. A comprehensive paediatric history, developmental review, and specialist formulation can be conducted via video call to consultant standard. Where physical examination is needed, your paediatrician will advise and coordinate in-person assessment with an appropriate specialist.What if my child needs to be seen in person?If your paediatrician determines that in-person assessment is needed, for physical examination, specific investigations, or acute assessment, they will advise clearly and coordinate the appropriate referral. Where urgency is indicated, your paediatrician will ensure you are directed to the appropriate service promptly.Can I get a second opinion on my child's diagnosis?Yes. Second opinion consultations are a core part of this service. Whether you want an independent review of a diagnosis, a management plan, or an investigation finding, including opinions received from other paediatricians or in another country, your paediatrician will conduct a full assessment and provide a clear, evidence-based independent perspective.Is this service suitable for newborns and very young infants?Yes. For non-emergency concerns. Fever in infants under 3 months of age is a medical emergency requiring immediate in-person assessment. Call 999 or attend your nearest emergency department. For other concerns in young infants, feeding difficulties, growth concerns, neonatal follow-up, this service is appropriate and your paediatrician will advise if in-person assessment is needed.Can developmental concerns such as autism or ADHD be assessed via video?Yes. For specialist assessment and formulation at consultant level. A comprehensive developmental history, observation during the video call, review of existing reports, and specialist clinical formulation can all be conducted remotely. Formal diagnostic assessment for autism typically requires a multidisciplinary team process. Your paediatrician will advise on the appropriate diagnostic pathway and coordinate referral where indicated.Will the paediatrician communicate with my child's GP?Yes, with your consent.
