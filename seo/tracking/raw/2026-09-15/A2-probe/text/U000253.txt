We specifically serve expat and international families who do not have a registered paediatrician or GP managing their child's care.Can I use this service for a newborn or very young infant?Yes, with an important caveat. Fever in infants under 3 months of age is a medical emergency and requires immediate in-person assessment. Call 999 or attend your nearest emergency department. For other non-emergency concerns in young infants, this service is appropriate and your doctor will advise clearly if in-person assessment is needed.Does my child need to be present during the consultation?For younger children, your presence as the parent or caregiver is what matters most. Your doctor will assess based on your description of symptoms and what is visible during the video call. For older children and adolescents, having the child present where possible improves the quality of the assessment.What if my child needs a physical examination?Some concerns require in-person physical assessment that cannot be adequately conducted via video call. If your doctor determines this is the case, they will advise you clearly and direct you to the most appropriate in-person service, including your nearest GP, paediatric outpatient clinic, or emergency department depending on urgency.Can I get a school absence certificate through this service?Yes. Medical certificates for school absence may be issued where clinically appropriate and at the doctor's professional discretion following full assessment.Is this service suitable for adolescents who want to speak to a doctor privately?Yes. Adolescent patients aged 16 and over may be assessed for capacity to consent in accordance with Irish medical standards. Your doctor will handle adolescent consultations with the appropriate level of confidentiality and sensitivity.What languages are consultations available in?English, Portuguese, Spanish, Czech, and Romanian.
