Your doctor carries out an assessment of your child's symptoms, relevant medical history, and current health status — with you present as parent or guardian throughout the consultation.Clear Explanation of Findings. Based on the assessment, your doctor advises on appropriate management — including guidance for home care, safety advice, referral for tests, or referral to specialist paediatrics when the presentation falls outside the scope of primary care. Every paediatric consultation includes clear safety advice — specific guidance on what to watch for, when to seek further care, and what would indicate your child needs emergency assessment. It is suitable for most common childhood illnesses and acute symptoms. For complex, chronic, or developmental conditions, we recommend our Paediatric Specialist Consultation, with a paediatrician registered with the Paediatrics College.Do I need an assigned family doctor in Portugal to use this service for my child?No. This service is accessible to all families in Portugal, regardless of NHS (SNS) registration status. We specifically serve international and expat families without an assigned family doctor managing their child's health.Can I use this service for a newborn or very young baby?Yes, with one important caveat. Fever in babies under 3 months old is a medical emergency and requires immediate in-person assessment — call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. For other non-urgent concerns in young babies, this service is suitable and your doctor advises clearly if in-person assessment is needed.Does my child need to be present during the consultation?For younger children, your presence as parent or guardian matters most — the doctor assesses based on your description of the symptoms and what's visible during the video call. For older children and teenagers, having the child present, whenever possible, improves the quality of the assessment.What if my child has a more complex condition than expected?If during the consultation the doctor identifies that your child's situation requires specialist assessment — because it is complex, chronic, or developmental — they advise you clearly and refer you to our Paediatric Specialist Consultation or to the most appropriate in-person service.Can I get a school certificate through this service?Yes. Medical certificates for school absence can be issued when clinically appropriate and at the doctor's professional discretion, after a complete assessment.Is this service suitable for teenagers who want to speak to a doctor privately?Yes. Teenage patients aged 16 and over can be assessed for capacity to consent, in line with Portuguese medical standards.
