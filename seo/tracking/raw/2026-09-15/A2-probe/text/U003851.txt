A management plan that does not correctly identify the mechanism is unlikely to be effective.An anaesthesiologist specialising in pain medicine brings a specific clinical framework to pain assessment Musculoskeletal pain arises from damage or dysfunction of muscles, joints, and connective tissues. The distinction matters clinically because the two types respond to different treatments — a management plan effective for musculoskeletal pain is unlikely to work for neuropathic pain, and vice versa.
