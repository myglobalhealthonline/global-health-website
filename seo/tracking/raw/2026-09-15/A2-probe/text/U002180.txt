Particularly for patients who are new to Ireland, unfamiliar with HSE services, or unsure what level of support they need. Your doctor will provide clear guidance on available services, how to access them, and what to expect.A Note on ConfidentialityEverything discussed during your mental health consultation is confidential and protected under Irish and EU data protection law.There are two situations in which confidentiality may need to be carefully considered: where there is a serious and immediate risk to your life or safety, or where there is a risk to the safety of another person. Your doctor will discuss confidentiality with you at the start of your consultation and will always approach any such situation with sensitivity, respect, and clinical care.Mental Health and Physical Health: The ConnectionMental and physical health are not separate systems. Anxiety and depression significantly affect cardiovascular health, immune function, metabolic health, and chronic disease management. Chronic physical conditions, pain, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, significantly increase the risk of depression and anxiety.A GP is uniquely positioned to assess both dimensions simultaneously. Identifying where mental health symptoms are driving physical symptoms, where physical conditions are contributing to mental health difficulties, and where an integrated management approach is needed.This is one of the most significant advantages of a GP-led mental health assessment over a standalone psychological assessment in many cases.Support for Expats and International ResidentsMental health challenges are disproportionately common among expats and international residents. Driven by isolation, cultural adjustment, language barriers, family separation, and the cumulative stress of building a life in a new country.Accessing mental health support in Ireland as an international resident is additionally challenging. Unfamiliarity with the HSE system, lack of a registered GP, language barriers, and cultural differences in how mental health is understood and discussed all create barriers to care.Our multi-lingual doctors provide mental health assessments in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Czech, and Romanian, and are experienced in the specific mental health challenges facing international communities in Ireland.Why Patients Choose UsSame-day accessMental health concerns do not follow a schedule. Our doctors assess your current mental health, provide clinical recommendations, and refer you to the appropriate level of support, including counselling, psychotherapy, or psychiatry where indicated. Counselling and psychotherapy sessions are not provided through this service.What if I need to see a psychiatrist?If your assessment indicates that psychiatric evaluation is needed, your doctor will advise you clearly and coordinate a referral to the appropriate specialist service. Public or private, depending on your situation and clinical urgency.Will my employer or anyone else be told about my consultation?No. Your consultation is completely confidential and protected under Irish and EU data protection law. Information is not shared without your explicit consent except in the specific circumstances discussed in the confidentiality section above.Can I use this service if I am already seeing a counsellor or therapist?Yes. A GP mental health assessment can complement ongoing counselling or therapy. Particularly where you need medical assessment, clinical recommendations, or coordination with other aspects of your healthcare.Is this service suitable for adolescents?Mental health consultations for patients under 16 require a parent or legal guardian to be present and to provide consent. For adolescents aged 16 and over, the doctor will assess capacity to consent in accordance with Irish medical and legal standards. For complex adolescent mental health concerns, specialist CAMHS referral may be more appropriate.What if I am in crisis during or after the consultation?If you are in crisis, please use the crisis resources listed at the top of this page.
