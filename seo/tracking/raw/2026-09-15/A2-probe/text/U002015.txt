They conduct formal psychological assessment and evidence-based therapy. Counsellors and psychotherapists provide talk-based therapeutic support but are not currently subject to statutory regulation in Ireland. Our practitioners are CORU-registered psychologists.How many sessions will I need?This depends on your presentation and therapeutic goals. Brief evidence-based interventions, such as CBT for specific anxiety disorders, may achieve significant improvement in 8 to 16 sessions. More complex or longstanding difficulties may require longer-term work. Your psychologist will discuss the expected treatment duration during the initial assessment and review this as therapy progresses.Is online psychological therapy as effective as in-person therapy?Yes. For the majority of presentations. A substantial body of research demonstrates that online CBT and other evidence-based therapies produce outcomes equivalent to in-person therapy for anxiety, depression, PTSD, and many other conditions. The therapeutic relationship, consistently identified as the strongest predictor of outcomes, is fully established in the online format.Can I get a formal ADHD assessment through this service?Yes. Formal psychological assessment for adult ADHD is available through our psychology service. This involves multiple assessment sessions, validated rating scales, clinical interview, and a formal written report with diagnostic conclusions and recommendations. Discuss this specifically when booking so the appropriate structure can be allocated.Will my psychologist communicate with my GP?With your consent, your psychologist can share a clinical summary with your GP. Supporting integrated care and ensuring your GP has the relevant clinical context. This is particularly important where your psychological difficulties have a physical health dimension or where medication review is relevant.Can I use this service if I am already seeing a psychiatrist?Yes. Psychological therapy and psychiatric management frequently work best in combination. Addressing both the psychological and biological dimensions of mental health. Your psychologist will communicate with your psychiatrist where clinically relevant and with your consent.Is this service suitable for adolescents?Psychology consultations for patients under 16 require parental or guardian consent and presence for the initial assessment. Adolescent psychological therapy requires specific clinical considerations. Your psychologist will advise on suitability and approach during the initial assessment.What if my difficulties require more intensive support than outpatient psychology?If your presentation requires more intensive support, including day hospital, inpatient care, or specialist services, your psychologist will advise clearly and coordinate appropriate referral. Your safety and clinical appropriateness of care always take priority.How do I book an online psychology consultation in Ireland?Choose the psychology service, select an available psychologist and appointment time, and complete the booking. Find a private place for the secure video consultation and share any relevant referral letters or previous assessment summaries in advance. This is a planned appointment, not crisis care.
