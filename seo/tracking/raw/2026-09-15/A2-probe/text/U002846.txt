Our doctors carry out a complete clinical assessment of the medical, hormonal, and metabolic factors contributing to your weight, and develop a personalised management plan based on the clinical findings. It is not a diet plan, a meal-replacement product, or a commercial weight loss product.Can anyone access GLP-1 therapy through this service?No. GLP-1 receptor agonist therapies are assessed as part of a complete clinical assessment. Eligibility is determined by clinical criteria — including BMI, weight-related conditions, contraindications, and general health status — and the decision is made exclusively by the doctor, after a complete assessment. Not every patient is clinically eligible.What if I've already tried GLP-1 therapy elsewhere?Bring any relevant clinical history, previous assessments, and treatment records to your consultation. Your doctor reviews your complete history and advises on the most appropriate next steps, based on your current clinical status and response to treatment.What tests might be needed?Depending on your clinical presentation, your doctor may request blood tests — including thyroid function, fasting glucose, HbA1c, hormone profile, and lipid profile — as part of the weight management assessment. Test requests can be coordinated at the same consultation or at a follow-up.Is this service suitable for patients with diabetes?Yes. Weight management in patients with type 2 diabetes requires integrated medical oversight — addressing metabolic control and weight simultaneously. Your doctor assesses your complete diabetic and metabolic profile as part of the consultation.How many consultations will I need?It depends on your individual clinical picture. An initial consultation establishes your baseline and management plan. Follow-up consultations are recommended to monitor progress, review response to treatment, and adjust the plan. Your doctor advises on the review schedule appropriate to your situation.Is this service suitable for teenagers?Weight management consultations are primarily for adults.
