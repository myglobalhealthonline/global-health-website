A detailed clinical history is the most powerful diagnostic tool in musculoskeletal medicine, and a complete history can be taken by video call. Your doctor also observes your movement, posture, and functional ability during the call. When physical examination findings are needed that cannot be assessed remotely, your doctor advises on the appropriate in-person assessment.What if I need an MRI or X-ray?If imaging is clinically indicated based on the assessment, your doctor issues a formal test request on the same day. You can then book the test at an imaging centre of your choice. Results can be reviewed at a follow-up consultation.Can I be referred to a physiotherapist or specialist?Yes. Referral letters for physiotherapy and specialist referrals — including orthopaedics, rheumatology, and neurology — are issued when clinically indicated, following assessment.What is cauda equina syndrome and why is it mentioned on this page?Cauda equina syndrome is a rare but serious spinal emergency, caused by compression of the nerve roots at the base of the spine. It presents as lower back pain associated with a change in bladder or bowel function, and requires emergency surgical assessment. It's mentioned on this page because it can be mistaken for common lower back pain. If you have lower back pain associated with any change in bladder or bowel function, go to the nearest emergency department immediately — do not wait for a video consultation.Is this service suitable for sports injuries?Yes. Sports and exercise-related musculoskeletal injuries are a core part of this service — including acute injuries, overuse conditions, and assessment for return to sport after injury.Is this service suitable for chronic pain conditions like fibromyalgia?Yes. Fibromyalgia and chronic pain syndromes benefit significantly from structured clinical assessment and a coordinated management plan. Your doctor assesses your complete clinical picture and advises on an integrated approach — including referral to physiotherapy, lifestyle guidance, and specialist referral when indicated.Do I need to have already had imaging done before booking?No. Bring any existing tests or clinical letters, if you have them — your doctor reviews them in full. If you haven't had imaging done yet, your doctor assesses whether it's clinically indicated and requests it when appropriate.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
