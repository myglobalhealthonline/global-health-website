For the majority of presentations. A detailed clinical history is the most powerful diagnostic tool in musculoskeletal medicine, and a thorough history can be taken via video call. Your doctor will also observe your movement, posture, and functional capacity during the call. Where physical examination findings that cannot be assessed remotely are needed, your doctor will advise on appropriate in-person assessment.What if I need an MRI or X-ray?If imaging is clinically indicated based on your assessment, your doctor will issue a formal investigation request on the same day. You can then book your imaging at a private imaging centre of your choice. Results can be reviewed in a follow-up consultation.Can you refer me to a physiotherapist or specialist?Yes. Physiotherapy referral letters and specialist referrals, including orthopaedics, rheumatology, and neurology, are issued where clinically indicated following assessment.What is cauda equina syndrome and why is it flagged on this page?Cauda equina syndrome is a rare but serious spinal emergency caused by compression of the nerve roots at the base of the spine. It presents as back pain alongside bladder or bowel dysfunction and requires emergency surgical assessment. It is flagged on this page because it can be mistaken for ordinary back pain. If you have back pain and any change in bladder or bowel function, attend your nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a video consultation.Is this service suitable for sports injuries?Yes. Sports and exercise-related musculoskeletal injuries are a core part of this service, including acute injuries, overuse conditions, and return-to-sport assessment following injury.Is this service suitable for chronic pain conditions like fibromyalgia?Yes. Fibromyalgia and chronic pain syndromes benefit significantly from structured clinical assessment and a coordinated management plan. Your doctor will assess your full clinical picture and advise on an integrated approach, including physiotherapy referral, lifestyle guidance, and specialist referral where indicated.Do I need to have had imaging done before booking?No. Bring any existing imaging or clinical letters if you have them. Your doctor will review them in full. If you have not had imaging, your doctor will assess whether it is clinically indicated and request it where appropriate.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
