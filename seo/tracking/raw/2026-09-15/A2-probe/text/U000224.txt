The Health and Social Care Professionals Council. Dietitians hold a recognised degree in dietetics and are clinically trained for therapeutic dietary management in acute medical settings. Nutritionists hold qualifications in nutritional science and provide evidence-based nutritional assessment and guidance. Our nutritionist holds a recognised qualification in nutritional science and provides comprehensive, evidence-based nutritional support across a wide range of health conditions and goals.Do I need a GP referral to book this service?No. You can book a nutrition consultation independently. If you have been referred by your GP or specialist, bring any relevant clinical letters or results to your consultation. Your nutritionist will review these in full.How many sessions will I need?This depends on your goals and health status. Many patients benefit from an initial assessment consultation followed by one or two follow-up sessions to review progress and adjust the plan. Patients managing complex chronic conditions or making significant dietary changes may benefit from more regular ongoing support. Your nutritionist will advise on the appropriate follow-up schedule during your initial consultation.Can nutritional changes really make a difference to my chronic condition?Yes. For many conditions, significantly. The evidence base for nutritional intervention in type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, IBS, PCOS, and inflammatory conditions is substantial. Nutritional optimisation is not a replacement for medical management, but it frequently enhances clinical outcomes significantly when integrated with appropriate medical care.Is this service suitable for children?Yes. Paediatric nutrition, from infant feeding and fussy eating to growth concerns and food allergy management, is a core part of this service. Consultations for children are conducted with a parent or caregiver present throughout.Can I use this service if I have food allergies?Yes. Food allergy and intolerance management is a core area of this service, including ensuring nutritional adequacy on restricted diets, structured elimination and reintroduction, and practical strategies for managing food allergies in daily life.Can this service help with sports performance?Yes. Sports and performance nutrition, covering pre- and post-training nutrition, competition nutrition, recovery, hydration, and supplement assessment, is a core part of this service for athletes and active individuals at all levels.What if I need blood tests to assess my nutritional status?Where nutritional blood tests are clinically indicated, ferritin, vitamin D, B12, zinc, or others, your nutritionist will advise on appropriate testing. Investigation requests can be coordinated through our GP Referral & Diagnostic Investigation service.Can I book an online nutrition consultation in Ireland?Yes. Choose the nutrition service, select an available practitioner and appointment time, and complete the booking. During the secure video consultation, the nutrition specialist will discuss your health history, current diet, symptoms and goals, then explain practical next steps they can help with.
