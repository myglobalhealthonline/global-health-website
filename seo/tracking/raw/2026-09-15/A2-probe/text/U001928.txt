From anywhere. Anytime.About UsGlobal Health is a telemedicine platform already operating in Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Romania, Czechia, and Brazil. Our promise is simple: Medicine Anytime, Anywhere. We're building a clinic that removes barriers between doctors and the patients who need them — wherever they are.Why Online Geriatric Medicine Is Needed in Ireland Right NowIreland's population aged 65 and over is projected to grow from 0.78 million in 2022 to more than 1.3 million by 2040 — a demographic shift that policymakers themselves describe as making rising demand for elderly care services inevitable. Ireland's overall waiting lists, covering inpatient, outpatient, and endoscopy services, reached 894,369 by the end of 2025, with staff constraints and population ageing repeatedly cited together as the primary drivers.For older patients, especially those with reduced mobility, travelling to an in-person appointment can itself be a significant barrier to care. Remote consultation is particularly well suited to this population — regular follow-up, medication review, and coordination of care for multiple chronic conditions can often be managed effectively without requiring a physical visit.Online geriatric medicine is one real way to help meet this growing need — and you can be part of it.The Position: Geriatrician for Online ConsultationsWe're looking for licensed geriatricians based in Ireland to join our online clinic. Through our platform, you'll provide remote geriatric consultations to patients and their families/carers across the country, with full flexibility to shape your work schedule.
100% remote — consult from anywhere, via video, with carers able to join when helpful
Flexible schedule — choose the hours and days that suit you
Main or supplementary role — ideal alongside an existing practice or hospital position
E-prescribing fully integrated — including polypharmacy review and medication adjustment, no extra paperwork
What You'll Be Part Of
A genuinely international team of doctors — collaborate and connect with colleagues across Europe and Brazil
A growing platform — you're joining Global Health at a growth stage in Ireland, not a position within an already-established system
Real impact — bring specialist geriatric care to older patients with reduced mobility, without requiring them to travel
Ongoing support — technical, administrative, and clinical support so you can focus on medicine, not paperwork
Who We're Looking For
Valid medical registration in Ireland (Irish Medical Council)
Specialisation in Geriatric Medicine
Experience with telemedicine tools is a plus (training and onboarding support provided)
Fluent English; Portuguese, Spanish, Romanian, or Czech are a bonus given our presence in those markets
Why NowWith the population aged 65 and over set to grow by more than 60% by 2040, and waiting lists already under significant pressure, the need for accessible geriatric care in Ireland will only intensify. Doctors who join at this stage help shape how online geriatric medicine actually works within Global Health in Ireland — this isn't just about filling a schedule, it's about building something new.
