For the vast majority of presentations. Dermatology is one of the medical specialties most suited to visual assessment, and a video consultation allows your doctor to observe the lesion, rash, or skin change directly. Good lighting and a clear camera view significantly improve assessment quality. Where a condition cannot be adequately assessed remotely, suspicious lesions requiring dermoscopy, conditions requiring biopsy or patch testing, your doctor will advise clearly and coordinate appropriate in-person referral.Can I get isotretinoin (Roaccutane) through this service?For moderate to severe acne, isotretinoin may be appropriate following full assessment. It requires structured monitoring, including pregnancy testing where relevant, and regular blood tests for liver function and lipids, with prescriptions issued for a defined period and renewed only after review at each follow-up appointment. Your doctor will explain what this involves during your consultation.I have a mole that has changed: how urgent is this?Changed moles warrant prompt clinical assessment. Book a consultation and show your doctor the lesion during the video call. Your doctor will assess using standard clinical criteria and advise on whether urgent in-person dermatology assessment is needed. If you are concerned, do not wait. Book a same-day appointment.Can I use this service for my child's eczema?Yes. Eczema in infants and children is one of the most common paediatric skin presentations and is a core part of this service. Your doctor will assess your child's eczema with you present throughout and advise on flare management, emollient regimen, trigger avoidance, and ongoing care.Can I get a dermatology referral through this service?Yes. Where specialist dermatology input is clinically indicated, including for suspicious lesions, severe or treatment-resistant conditions, or conditions requiring procedures, your doctor will issue a formal referral letter on the same day, addressed to a dermatologist of your choice or the appropriate specialist service.What if I have already been seen by a dermatologist and need ongoing GP management?Yes. Bring any existing letters, reports, or treatment plans from your dermatologist to your consultation. Your doctor will review these in full and manage your ongoing care at GP level, coordinating with your specialist as needed.Is this service suitable for fungal skin infections?Yes. Fungal skin infections, including tinea corporis, tinea pedis, tinea versicolor, and scalp fungal infections, are assessed and managed at GP level. Your doctor will assess the presentation and advise on appropriate treatment.What if my rash is spreading rapidly or I feel unwell alongside it?A rapidly spreading rash accompanied by fever, difficulty breathing, facial swelling, or feeling seriously unwell is a medical emergency. Call 999 or attend your nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a video consultation.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
