Chronic Disease follow-up is ongoing care — a doctor who knows your case over time, monitors your condition, adjusts management when necessary, and is involved in your long-term health.How often will I need to have a consultation?It depends on your condition and how well controlled it is. For stable and well-controlled conditions, a quarterly or semiannual review is generally appropriate. For less well-controlled or more complex conditions, more frequent review is needed. Your doctor will advise you on the appropriate review schedule at the first consultation.Can the doctor access my Spanish medical history?No. The doctors at Global Health do not have access to the electronic medical record system of the Spanish public health system. Please bring any relevant medical documentation — previous blood test results, specialist letters, imaging reports, and a list of your current medication — to your first appointment.What happens if I need a specialist?When specialized evaluation is needed, your doctor issues a formal referral letter and advises you on access to the appropriate specialist in Spain. Access to private specialists is generally straightforward in Spain’s major cities — Global Health coordinates this as part of ongoing follow-up.Can I use this service if I have a primary care doctor in Spain?Yes. Some patients use Global Health for follow-up consultations for chronic diseases in English or Portuguese alongside their Spanish GP record — especially for conditions where they want to discuss management in detail in a language they fully understand.What happens if I need emergency care for my chronic illness?The management of chronic disease emergencies — hypertensive crisis, diabetic emergency, acute cardiac event — requires immediate emergency care. Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Ongoing follow-up through Global Health is for non-urgent chronic disease care.Do I need Spanish health insurance?No. It is a private direct-pay service. The clinical documentation issued through this service is dispensed at the full private price regardless of insurance status.Can I use this service from anywhere in Spain?Yes — including the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.
