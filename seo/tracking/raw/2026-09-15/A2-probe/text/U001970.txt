Our Irish-registered doctors take a full history, look at your throat and breathing on camera, and tell you what this is, what to do about it, and what would make it urgent.What we assess by videoChest infections and bronchitisA cough that has not settled after two to three weeksSore throat and tonsillitisSinusitis and persistent nasal congestionFlu, COVID-19 and other viral illnessAsthma or COPD symptoms that have worsenedWhen you need to be seen in person insteadVideo has limits, and we are direct about them. Go to an emergency department or call 112/999 if you have severe breathlessness at rest, chest pain, blue lips, confusion or drowsiness, or if you are coughing blood. A video consultation is not the right route for those, and your doctor will say so immediately if any of it comes up.Antibiotics are not automaticMost respiratory infections are viral, and antibiotics do nothing for them. Your doctor prescribes where the assessment points to a bacterial infection, and explains the reasoning either way — including what should improve, by when, and when to come back.
