The formal sick note corresponds to the Temporary Incapacity for Work Certificate, with notification to Social Security, and is linked to sickness benefit for longer periods of incapacity. For most short-term illness situations, the medical certificate of absence is sufficient — but if your condition justifies a longer period, your doctor can issue a formal sick note.Will my employer accept the medical certificate issued through this service?Yes. Medical certificates issued by our doctors, registered with the Portuguese Medical Association, are accepted by most employers and educational institutions in Portugal as valid proof of illness.Can I request a formal sick note directly instead of a certificate of absence?You can mention your preference during the consultation, but the final decision on which documentation is clinically appropriate rests with the doctor, based on the assessment carried out. If your situation does not justify a formal sick note, the doctor explains the reasons and proposes the appropriate documentation.Can I get retroactive documentation?Retroactive documentation is not usually issued, since our doctors cannot clinically assess a period of illness they did not directly observe. If you were unwell previously and need related documentation, discuss the situation with the doctor during the consultation.Is the documentation guaranteed after the consultation?No. The decision on what documentation to issue is a clinical judgement made by the doctor, based on the assessment carried out. If the clinical findings do not justify any documentation, the doctor informs you of the appropriate next steps.How quickly do I receive my document?When issued, the document is processed electronically on the same day as the consultation. In the case of a formal sick note, notification to Social Security is done directly by the doctor.Can I use this service if I am self-employed or a freelancer?Yes.
