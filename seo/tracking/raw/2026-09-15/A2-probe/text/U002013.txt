The therapeutic value of physiotherapy has always been primarily in what the patient does between sessions, not what happens during them.Online physiotherapy is not a compromise. For assessment, exercise prescription, and rehabilitation guidance, it is a clinically valid and in many cases more accessible, more convenient, and more cost-effective alternative to in-person care.Where hands-on treatment is genuinely needed, for specific manual therapy or joint mobilisation, your physiotherapist will tell you clearly and direct you to the appropriate in-person service.Why Patients Choose UsCORU-registered physiotherapistOur physiotherapist is registered with CORU. For assessment, exercise prescription, and rehabilitation guidance, which are the core clinical components of physiotherapy. Your physiotherapist will observe your movement, posture, and functional capacity via video and prescribe a personalised rehabilitation programme based on a thorough clinical assessment. Where hands-on manual therapy is specifically needed, your physiotherapist will advise and direct you to the appropriate in-person service.Will I need equipment for my exercise programme?Your physiotherapist will design your programme around what you have available. Whether that is a fully equipped gym, a resistance band and a mat, or nothing at all. Equipment is never a barrier to starting rehabilitation.How many sessions will I need?This depends on your condition, its complexity, and your rehabilitation goals. Acute conditions with straightforward rehabilitation needs may resolve with two to four sessions. Complex, longstanding, or post-surgical conditions may require more structured ongoing support. Your physiotherapist will advise on the appropriate follow-up schedule during your initial consultation and review this as your rehabilitation progresses.Can I use this service for pelvic floor problems?Yes. Pelvic floor dysfunction, including stress incontinence, urgency, prolapse, and postnatal pelvic floor recovery, is a core part of this service. Your physiotherapist will conduct a pelvic floor assessment appropriate to the remote format and prescribe a structured pelvic floor rehabilitation programme. Where an internal examination is clinically indicated, your physiotherapist will advise on in-person assessment.Is online physiotherapy covered by health insurance?Health insurance coverage for online physiotherapy varies between providers and plans. We recommend checking with your insurer before booking. We can provide a receipt and clinical summary following your consultation for insurance claim purposes.Can I use this service alongside in-person physiotherapy I am already receiving?Yes. Our online physiotherapy consultation can complement in-person physiotherapy. For example, providing a second opinion on your current programme, supporting your home exercise compliance, or bridging between in-person sessions.Can this service help with post-surgical rehabilitation?Yes. For the exercise prescription and rehabilitation guidance component of post-surgical recovery. Your physiotherapist will design a structured rehabilitation programme appropriate to your surgical procedure and recovery stage. Where hands-on treatment is indicated as part of your post-surgical pathway, your physiotherapist will advise on the appropriate in-person service.Is this service suitable for children?Paediatric physiotherapy, including developmental motor concerns, sports injuries in young athletes, and respiratory conditions, can be assessed through this service. Consultations for children are conducted with a parent or caregiver present throughout.How do I book an online physiotherapy consultation in Ireland?Choose the physiotherapy service, select an available practitioner and appointment time, and complete the booking. For the secure video consultation, wear clothing that lets you move comfortably and have enough space for the practitioner to observe relevant movement. Keep any scan reports, operation notes or current exercise plan nearby.
