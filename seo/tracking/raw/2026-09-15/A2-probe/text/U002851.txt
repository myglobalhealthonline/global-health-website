Seeking a second opinion is a clinically encouraged and ethically supported practice. The Portuguese Medical Association explicitly supports a patient's right to seek an independent clinical perspective. Your current doctor shouldn't take offence — and if they do, that in itself is clinically significant.Will the second opinion necessarily differ from the first?Not necessarily — and an honest second opinion that confirms the original diagnosis is just as valuable as one that differs. Confirmation with understanding is clinically significant. What you're seeking is an independent perspective from a doctor who has reviewed the same evidence without prior assumptions — whatever that perspective turns out to be.Can your doctors review reports from specialist doctors?Yes — at primary-care level. Our doctors can review specialist letters, consultant reports, and clinical documentation, and provide a primary-care-level perspective on the clinical reasoning, diagnosis, and management plan. When a genuine specialist-level second opinion is warranted — for complex surgical decisions, oncology management, or other specialist areas — your doctor advises and coordinates the appropriate specialist referral.Can I use this service if my clinical documentation is in another language?Yes. Our doctors can review documentation in English, Spanish, Czech, and Romanian, as well as Portuguese. If your documents are in another language, bring them anyway and your doctor works with what's available, together with your account of what you were told.What if the second opinion raises concerns about my original diagnosis?If your doctor's independent assessment raises questions about your original diagnosis or management plan, they explain clearly what the concerns are, what the evidence supports, and what the appropriate next steps are — including whether further investigation or specialist review is warranted. Your doctor doesn't alarm you without clinical basis — but is honest with you about what the clinical picture suggests.Can I get a second opinion on a mental health diagnosis?Yes — at primary-care level. Our doctors can provide an independent primary-care-level perspective on a mental health diagnosis, management plan, or medication approach. For specialist psychiatric second opinions, your doctor advises on the appropriate referral.Can I use this service to get a second opinion on a diagnosis received abroad?Yes — this is one of the most common uses of this service. Bring any relevant clinical documentation from your home country and your doctor reviews it in full, providing an independent perspective in the Portuguese and European clinical context.What is the difference between this and a standard GP Consultation?A GP Consultation is for assessing new or current symptoms. A Second Opinion Consultation is specifically structured around reviewing an existing diagnosis, clinical letter, or management plan — with dedicated time for reviewing documentation, discussing clinical reasoning, and providing an evidence-based assessment of what you've been told. It's a different clinical task requiring a different structure.Also available inPortugueseSpanishCzechRomanianGermanMedical disclaimer.
