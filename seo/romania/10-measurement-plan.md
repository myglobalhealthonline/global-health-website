# Romania measurement design

Dated 13 September 2026. This records the measurement method; dates and operational
actions remain in the global ledger. There is no deployment date yet.

Baseline: final GSC pages, 13 August–9 September versus 16 July–12 August, filtered
to `/romania/`, with all searcher countries. Preserve a separate Romanian-language
subset and a Romania-resident cut. Use complete pagination and stored UTC retrieval
timestamps. Query totals omit anonymized queries and cannot replace page totals.

Measure commercial services/profiles separately from tools, articles and hubs.
Primary indicators: relevant non-brand commercial impressions/clicks, query-to-page
ownership and locale alignment. Availability changes belong beside the performance
data: traffic to an unstaffed service is not a booking opportunity.

At publication, register the exact URL/locale/field cohort and date. After 28 complete
post-publication days, compare matched windows; review again at 60 and 90 days.
Avoid claiming causality from a small seven-click service baseline. Recheck newly
indexed pages and branded traffic separately, not through a blended average position.

Booking outcomes require verified analytics collection and privacy-safe conversion
events. This package does not assert new GA4 verification or recovered historical
data. Do not interpret zero events as zero bookings without validating measurement.

Technical readback immediately after each batch: HTTP status, actual title/meta/H1,
FAQ body/schema equivalence, no duplicated embedded FAQ, real hreflang targets,
correct booking language and unchanged price/service assignment. A successful write
is not proof of a successful public render.

Keep the existing September 24 global URL Inspection cadence. Add focused inspection
only when a changed cohort needs it; do not resubmit every country URL or use the
Google Indexing API for ordinary service pages.
