# Hero image prompts — Week 3 editorial batch, September 2026

One 16:9 hero per article, 6 total. The image carries no language, so any later
locale row reuses its market's hero. Same production pipeline as August:
generate at 2400×1350, `seed-blog-covers` caps width at 1600px on upload.
The calm-editorial hero renders the cover `object-contain` in a framed figure
under the title, so the composition must survive being seen whole, not cropped.

## Shared style spine (prepend to every prompt)

> Editorial healthcare photography, premium magazine quality. Natural window
> light, soft directional shadows, shallow depth of field. Muted palette: deep
> forest green, warm ivory, soft slate, one restrained accent. Calm, competent,
> human — never stocky, never smiling-at-camera, never a handshake. Real
> environments with lived-in detail. 16:9, 2400×1350.

## Shared negative constraints (append to every prompt)

> No legible text, no lettering, no numbers on documents or screens. No logos,
> no brand marks, no government emblems or official forms. No pill bottles, no
> syringes, no blood. No stethoscope draped over a keyboard. No clip-art icons,
> no infographic overlays, no watermarks. No distorted hands, no extra fingers.
> Not clinical-white and sterile.

## 1 · IE — Adult ADHD assessment (`ie-adhd-assessment`)

Subject: the long conversation, not the questionnaire. A consulting room in a
converted Georgian house in Dublin, two armchairs at a slight angle to each
other rather than across a desk. A clinician in their forties listening, pen
resting, a closed notebook on their knee; the patient, early thirties, seen
from behind the shoulder, hands loosely clasped. On a low table between them a
glass of water and a plain blank rating sheet turned face down. Tall sash
window, soft grey Irish daylight, bookshelves out of focus. The frame reads:
someone is finally being heard properly. Alt text: "Adult ADHD assessment
conversation between a clinician and a patient in a calm consulting room".

## 2 · PT — Baixa psicológica (`pt-baixa-psicologica`)

Subject: the morning the decision is made to stop. A person in their late
twenties sitting at a small kitchen table in a Lisbon apartment, laptop closed
and pushed to the side, a mug of coffee gone cold, phone face-up but dark. They
are looking out of the window, not at any screen. Strong Atlantic morning light
through half-open wooden shutters, pale tiled floor, a plant. Fatigue shown
through stillness, not tears. No documents visible. The story is rest chosen,
not collapse. Alt text: "Person resting at a kitchen table in Lisbon on the
first day of sick leave for mental health".

## 3 · CZ — Vycházky na neschopence (`cz-neschopenka-vychazky`)

Subject: the allowed hour outside. A person in a wool coat on a short walk in a
Prague residential street in late afternoon, one hand on a low garden gate,
about to go back in. Apartment building with a row of doorbells and nameplates
(blank, unreadable) beside the entrance in the near plane. Bare autumn trees,
low golden light on cobbles, a tram wire overhead. The clock is implied by the
light, not shown. Calm, unhurried, lawful. Alt text: "Person returning home
from a permitted walk during Czech sick leave, doorbell panel by the entrance".

## 4 · ES — Cómo bajar la tensión (`es-como-bajar-tension`)

Subject: the change that actually works, made ordinary. A kitchen counter in a
Valencia flat at midday: a wooden board with tomatoes, a lemon halved, a bunch
of parsley, olive oil in an unlabelled glass bottle, a salt cellar deliberately
pushed to the far edge of frame, half out of focus. A validated upper-arm blood
pressure cuff resting coiled beside a notebook with a pencil, no readable
numbers on its screen. Hands of a person in their fifties slicing. Warm
Mediterranean light, terracotta and ivory. No tea, no infusion, no herbs in
mugs. Alt text: "Mediterranean vegetables, olive oil and a home blood pressure
monitor on a kitchen counter".

## 5 · RO — Tensiune mică (`ro-tensiune-mica`)

Subject: the moment of sitting down, done right. A woman in her thirties seated
on the bottom step of a staircase in a Bucharest apartment building hallway,
feet flat, one hand on the wall, a full glass of water held in the other. Head
slightly bowed, eyes open, recovering, not fainted. A second person's hand
resting on her shoulder enters from frame edge. Cool daylight from a stairwell
window, painted plaster wall, worn wooden banister. Nothing dramatic: a pause,
water, someone nearby. Alt text: "Woman sitting down on a stair with a glass of
water after feeling dizzy from low blood pressure".

## 6 · BR — Atestado de comparecimento (`br-atestado-comparecimento`)

Subject: the two-hour appointment, seen from the reception desk on the way
out. A clinic reception in São Paulo, receptionist's hands stamping a small
plain card (blank, no text) on a light wooden counter and sliding it toward a
patient whose hand reaches in from the right, a wristwatch visible on the
patient's wrist. Beyond the counter, through glass, a busy street at midday
in soft focus, a motorbike, palms. Bright diffused light, ivory and green
interior. The document is small and quick; the day continues. Alt text:
"Clinic receptionist handing a stamped attendance declaration to a patient
in Brazil".
