# Hero image prompts — blog SEO batch, August 2026

One 16:9 hero per article, 12 total. Each locale row reuses its market's hero —
the image carries no language, so it works across all 6 locales.

## Shared style spine (prepend to every prompt)

> Editorial healthcare photography, premium magazine quality. Natural window
> light, soft directional shadows, shallow depth of field. Muted palette: deep
> forest green, warm ivory, soft slate, one restrained accent. Calm, competent,
> human — never stocky, never smiling-at-camera, never a handshake. Real
> environments with lived-in detail. 16:9, 2400×1350.

## Shared negative constraints (append to every prompt)

> No legible text, no lettering, no numbers on documents or screens. No logos,
> no brand marks, no government emblems or official forms. No pill bottles, no
> syringes pointed at the viewer, no blood. No stethoscope draped over a
> keyboard. No clip-art icons, no infographic overlays, no watermarks. No
> distorted hands, no extra fingers. Not clinical-white and sterile.

## 1 · IE — Illness Benefit Ireland (`ie-illness-benefit`)

Subject: the moment of claiming, seen from the kitchen table rather than the
office. A person in their thirties at a worn wooden table in an Irish terraced
house, laptop open at an angle away from camera, a plain unbranded printed form
beside a mug of tea, a phone face-down. Grey Dublin daylight through a sash
window with rain on the glass. Their posture is concentrated, mid-task, not
distressed. The frame reads: paperwork done from home while unwell.

## 2 · IE — Blood tests Dublin (`ie-blood-tests`)

Subject: the calm two minutes before a venous draw. Close three-quarter view of
a phlebotomist's gloved hands preparing a tourniquet and unlabelled collection
tubes on a stainless tray, patient's forearm resting relaxed on an armrest at
the edge of frame, faces out of focus. Warm lamp light against a Georgian
window. Tubes coloured by cap, arranged in a neat row — the visual cue for
"panel of tests". Sterile technique visible, nothing gruesome; no needle in skin.

## 3 · CZ — eNeschopenka (`cz-neschopenka`)

Subject: the paperless sick note. A Czech GP's consulting room, doctor's hands
at a desktop keyboard sending a record, an empty prescription pad pushed aside
and closed — the retired paper. Second plane: a patient in a coat seated,
already standing to leave, coat still on. Cool northern light, panelled wall,
a mug and a desk lamp. The story is the transfer of a document without paper
changing hands.

## 4 · CZ — Doctor online 24/7 (`cz-lekar-online`)

Subject: care at an hour when nothing else is open. A parent in a dim Czech
flat at night, seated on the sofa, tablet propped on a knee showing a soft
blurred video-call glow on their face, a sleeping child's blanket in the near
foreground. One warm lamp, blue night at the window, city rooftops beyond.
Quiet, contained, not panicked. The light on the face comes from the screen.

## 5 · PT — Autodeclaração de doença (`pt-autodeclaracao`)

Subject: self-declaration, done in a minute, from bed. Over-the-shoulder of a
person sitting on the edge of a bed in a sunlit Lisbon bedroom, phone in hand
with an unreadable simple form on screen, a glass of water and a folded blanket
beside them. Azulejo-tiled wall detail, strong Atlantic light, pale linen.
Convalescent but functional — the point is a small administrative act, not
serious illness.

## 6 · PT — Consulta do viajante (`pt-consulta-viajante`)

Subject: preparation, weeks before the flight. A flat-lay-adjacent table scene
in a bright Porto flat: a half-packed rucksack, a plain unbranded booklet, a
folded map, insect-repellent bottle without a label, sun hat, and a doctor's
hand entering the frame placing a small vaccination-record card down. One
person mid-thirties out of focus behind. Warm afternoon light, travel intent
obvious, no airport, no aeroplane cliché.

## 7 · ES — Baja laboral por ansiedad (`es-baja-ansiedad`)

Subject: functional impairment, shown honestly and without stigma. A person at
a desk in a Madrid office at end of day, chair turned away from the monitor,
hands resting in lap, gaze towards the window rather than the screen. Empty
open-plan behind, blinds striping the light, monitor dark. Restraint is
essential: no head-in-hands, no clutched temples, no rain-on-window sadness.
The subject looks tired and stalled, not broken.

## 8 · ES — Dermatólogo online (`es-dermatologo-online`)

Subject: the photograph that makes the consultation work. Close view of a
person by a window photographing their own forearm with a phone in daylight, a
small ruler laid on the skin beside a faint benign patch for scale. Second
plane, out of focus: a laptop showing an unreadable image-review layout.
Natural light only, no flash, true skin tones. The frame teaches the technique
the article teaches. No alarming lesion, no melanoma imagery.

## 9 · RO — Scrisoarea medicală (`ro-scrisoare-medicala`)

Subject: one doctor writing to another, with the patient in the middle. A
Romanian consulting room, a specialist's hands completing a plain unbranded
two-page form, a stamp and pad resting nearby, a second identical sheet
underneath — the two copies. Patient's folder of older documents at the frame
edge. Warm tungsten and window light, older institutional desk, worn wood.
Documentary, unglamorous, precise.

## 10 · RO — Boli cronice / programe naționale (`ro-boli-cronice`)

Subject: the months between appointments, which is where the article lives. A
person in their sixties at a kitchen table in a Romanian flat, blood-pressure
cuff on the upper arm, a small notebook of handwritten readings open beside a
weekly pill organiser. Morning light, patterned tablecloth, a phone face-down.
Self-monitoring as routine and dignified — not a hospital, not a ward, no
drip stand.

## 11 · BR — Atestado médico online (`br-atestado-medico`)

Subject: a real consultation producing a real document. Split emphasis in one
frame: foreground, a person at home in São Paulo holding a phone showing an
unreadable PDF with a signature block and a verification code area; background,
soft and out of focus, the same person's laptop with a finished video call.
Warm late-afternoon light, plants, tiled floor. Authenticity is the theme —
signed document, not a stamped form. No rubber stamp anywhere in frame.

## 12 · BR — Solicitação de exames (`br-pedido-exames`)

Subject: the question before the test. A Brazilian family doctor's hands at a
laptop selecting items on an unreadable list, an older printed result sheet
spread beside it for comparison — past results informing the new request.
Second plane: an unlabelled specimen tube rack, deliberately small in the
frame, because the article's point is the reasoning and not the collection.
Warm daylight, wooden desk, notebook.

## Notes

- Reuse the same lens character and grade across all 12 so the blog index reads
  as one set: 35–50 mm equivalent, f/2.0–2.8, gentle film grain, no HDR.
- Every market's people should be plausibly local to that market.
- Where a document appears it must be visibly generic. Nothing in these images
  should imitate a CNAS, INSS, DSP, CFM or Seguridad Social form.
- Alt text should describe the scene, not repeat the headline.
