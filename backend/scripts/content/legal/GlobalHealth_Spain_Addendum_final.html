ES-2. Medical Regulation and Clinician Registration in Spain

Medical practice in Spain is regulated at national level by the Consejo General de Colegios Oficiales de Médicos (CGCOM) and at provincial level by the relevant Colegio de Médicos. All Clinicians providing consultations to patients in Spain through the Platform are registered with the relevant provincial Colegio de Médicos or hold equivalent EU professional qualifications recognised in Spain under Directive 2005/36/EC on the recognition of professional qualifications.

Each Clinician's registration details, including their Colegio de Médicos registration number and the province of registration, are displayed on their individual profile page on the Platform. Patients may verify a Clinician's registration via the CGCOM online register at www.cgcom.es.

Each Clinician providing services through the Platform in Spain:

  • Holds current registration with a Spanish Colegio de Médicos or holds EU-recognised professional qualifications pursuant to Directive 2005/36/EC.
  • Maintains their own professional indemnity or civil liability insurance (seguro de responsabilidad civil profesional) as required under Spanish law and by their Colegio de Médicos.
  • Practises in accordance with the ethical and deontological standards of the Organización Médica Colegial (OMC) and applicable Spanish healthcare law.
  • Is solely responsible for the clinical advice, diagnosis, and treatment recommendations provided during consultations.

ES-4. Consumer Rights in Spain

ES-4.1 Applicable consumer legislation

Your rights as a consumer in Spain are protected by the following legislation and cannot be excluded or limited by the Master Terms or this Addendum:

  • Real Decreto Legislativo 1/2007 — Texto Refundido de la Ley General para la Defensa de los Consumidores y Usuarios (TRLGDCU) — the consolidated Spanish Consumer Protection Act.
  • Ley 34/2002 — Ley de Servicios de la Sociedad de la Información y del Comercio Electrónico (LSSICE) — regulating online and information society services.
  • EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU — implemented in Spain through the TRLGDCU.
  • EU Directive 2011/24/EU — patient rights in cross-border healthcare.

ES-4.2 Statutory 14-day cooling-off period

For services booked online (distance contracts), you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal from the date of entering the contract, under Articles 102–108 TRLGDCU. You are not required to give any reason for withdrawal within this period.

If you expressly request that a consultation take place within the 14-day cooling-off period and the consultation is completed, you waive your right to cancel that specific consultation. By booking a consultation to occur within 14 days of the contract date, you expressly acknowledge and consent to this waiver.

This cooling-off right applies to individual consultation bookings and to Credit Plan subscriptions. For Credit Plans, the 14-day cooling-off period runs from the date of first subscription. If no Credits have been used during the cooling-off period, you are entitled to a full refund. If Credits have been used, a proportionate deduction reflecting the value of Credits used will apply.

ES-4.3 Credit Plan — no minimum term

Credit Plans available in Spain operate on a monthly rolling basis with no minimum contract term and no cancellation fee. You may cancel at any time in accordance with Clause 7.5 of the Master Terms. Your statutory rights under Spanish consumer law apply in addition to these contractual rights.

ES-4.4 Refunds

Refunds to which you are entitled under the Master Terms, this Addendum, or Spanish consumer law will be processed to your original payment method within 14 days of the refund decision being confirmed, in accordance with TRLGDCU.

ES-4.5 Emergency services

EMERGENCIA: In Spain, call 112 for emergency services. Do not use the Global Health Platform in a medical emergency.

ES-5. Data Protection in Spain

ES-5.1 Applicable law and supervisory authorities

Data processing for patients in Spain is governed by GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Ley Orgánica 3/2018 — Ley de Protección de Datos Personales y garantía de los derechos digitales (LOPDGDD) — which implements and supplements GDPR in Spain.

Global Guest s.r.o.'s lead supervisory authority under GDPR's one-stop-shop mechanism is the Czech data protection supervisory authority (ÚÔÚ). Patients in Spain also have the right to contact the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) at www.aepd.es. AEPD formal notification of Global Health's DPO appointment is in progress and will be completed prior to go-live.

ES-5.2 Data Protection Officer

Global Health has appointed Dr Ahmed Maklad as Data Protection Officer (DPO) for all markets in which Global Health operates, including Spain. The DPO can be contacted at dpo@myglobalhealth.online. Full DPO contact details are published in the Privacy Policy at www.myglobalhealth.online/privacy.

ES-5.3 Legal bases for processing Health Data

In the Platform Model, Global Health processes patient Health Data in a limited capacity as part of facilitating the booking and delivery of consultations. Processing is carried out on the following legal bases:

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — processing necessary for the performance of the contract between the patient and Global Health for the provision of platform services.
  • Article 9(2)(a) GDPR — your explicit consent for the processing of Health Data, obtained at registration and reaffirmed before each consultation.
  • Article 9(2)(h) GDPR — processing necessary for the purposes of medical diagnosis and the provision of health care, carried out by a health professional subject to professional secrecy obligations.

The treating Clinician processes Health Data as an independent data controller in their own right, subject to their own GDPR and professional confidentiality obligations.

ES-5.4 Clinical records

Consultation records generated through the Platform are held by the treating Clinician as the independent healthcare professional. Global Health holds booking, transactional, and platform interaction data. Patients wishing to access their clinical consultation records should contact the treating Clinician directly or submit a data access request to globalhealth@myglobalhealth.online and we will facilitate contact with the relevant Clinician.

ES-5.5 Data retention

Platform and transactional data is retained for a minimum of 5 years in accordance with applicable Spanish commercial and tax law. Clinical records are held by the individual Clinician in accordance with their professional obligations and applicable Spanish healthcare legislation, including Ley 41/2002 (Ley de Autonomía del Paciente).

ES-5.6 Financial records retention

Financial and billing records are retained for 5 years in accordance with Spanish commercial law (Código de Comercio) and 4 years under Spanish tax law (Ley General Tributaria).

Global Health is responsible for:

Each Clinician is solely responsible for:

Nothing in this clause excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or any other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded under Spanish consumer law or EU law.

Where Spanish mandatory consumer protection law (TRLGDCU) imposes terms or remedies more favourable to you than the above, those mandatory terms apply.
