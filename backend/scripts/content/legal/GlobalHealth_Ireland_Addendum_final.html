IE-2. Medical Regulation in Ireland

Medical practice in Ireland is regulated by the Irish Medical Council (IMC) under the Medical Practitioners Act 2007. All Clinicians providing consultations to patients in Ireland through the Platform hold current registration with the IMC.

Each Clinician's IMC registration number is displayed on their individual profile page on the Platform. Patients may verify a Clinician's registration at any time via the IMC online register at www.medicalcouncil.ie.

Dr Tiago Miguel Figueira, Clinical Lead for the Irish service, holds IMC registration number 523449.

All Clinicians providing services in Ireland comply with the Medical Council of Ireland's Guide to Professional Conduct and Ethics for Registered Medical Practitioners, including its provisions on telemedicine and remote prescribing.

Global Health maintains a clinical governance framework for Irish operations that includes: ongoing verification of Clinician IMC registration status; adherence to Medical Council telemedicine guidance; clinical audit processes; and a formal complaints and clinical incident review procedure.

IE-3. Prescription Services in Ireland

Prescription services in Ireland are regulated by the Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA) and are subject to the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) Regulations 2003 (as amended).

Prescriptions are issued only where clinically appropriate following a full consultation. All prescriptions are issued by IMC-registered doctors practicing in Ireland. The prescribing doctor's name and IMC registration number appear on each prescription.

Prescriptions for controlled drugs listed in Schedules 1 and 2 of the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 1988 (as amended), including opioids, cocaine-derived substances, and other drugs requiring special prescription formalities under Irish law, are not issued through the Platform. Prescriptions for Schedule 3 and 4 controlled drugs, including benzodiazepines and other medications routinely prescribed in general practice, may be issued where clinically appropriate following a full consultation and in accordance with the prescribing Clinician's professional judgment and applicable Irish Medical Council guidance.

Prescriptions issued by Global Health Clinicians in Ireland are valid in Ireland in accordance with applicable Irish medicines legislation. Patients intending to use a prescription outside Ireland must verify its validity with the relevant authority in the destination country. Global Health does not guarantee acceptance of Irish prescriptions by pharmacies or health systems outside Ireland.

Home prescription delivery services, where offered, are operated in compliance with applicable Irish medicines distribution regulations. Details of any delivery service are published on the Platform at the time of booking.

IE-4.2 Statutory 14-day cooling-off period

For services booked online (distance contracts), you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal from the date of entering the contract, in accordance with the European Union (Consumer Information, Cancellation and Other Rights) Regulations 2013. You are not required to give any reason for withdrawal within this period.

If you expressly request that a consultation take place within the 14-day cooling-off period and the consultation is completed, you waive your right to cancel that specific consultation. By booking a consultation to occur within 14 days of the contract date, you expressly acknowledge and consent to this waiver.

This cooling-off right applies to individual consultation bookings and to Credit Plan subscriptions. For Credit Plans, the 14-day cooling-off period runs from the date of first subscription. If no Credits have been used during the cooling-off period, you are entitled to a full refund. If Credits have been used, a proportionate deduction reflecting the value of Credits used will apply to any refund.

IE-4.3 Credit Plan — no minimum term

Credit Plans available in Ireland operate on a monthly rolling basis with no minimum contract term and no cancellation fee. You may cancel at any time in accordance with Clause 7.5 of the Master Terms. Your statutory cooling-off rights under Irish law apply in addition to these contractual rights.

IE-4.4 Refunds

Refunds to which you are entitled under the Master Terms, this Addendum, or Irish consumer law will be processed to your original payment method within 14 days of the date on which the right to refund is confirmed, in accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2022.

IE-5. Data Protection in Ireland

IE-5.1 Applicable law

Data processing for patients in Ireland is governed by GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Data Protection Acts 1988-2018. Global Guest s.r.o.'s lead supervisory authority under GDPR's one-stop-shop mechanism is the Czech data protection supervisory authority (ÚÔÚ). Global Health is also registered with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) as a data controller in respect of its Irish branch operations.

IE-5.2 Legal bases for processing Health Data

We process your Health Data in Ireland on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Article 9(2)(a) — your explicit consent, obtained at registration and reaffirmed before each consultation.
  • Article 9(2)(h) — processing necessary for the purposes of preventive medicine, medical diagnosis, the provision of health or social care or treatment, or the management of health or social care systems and services.

IE-5.3 Supervisory authority

The supervisory authority for data protection in Ireland is the Data Protection Commission (DPC), 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28. Website: www.dataprotection.ie. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the DPC at any time, in addition to or instead of raising concerns with us directly.

IE-5.4 Clinical records retention

Clinical and medical records for patients in Ireland are retained for a minimum of 8 years from the date of the consultation, in accordance with HSE National Standards for Healthcare Records Management and Medical Council of Ireland guidance.

Records relating to patients who were under 18 at the time of treatment are retained until the patient's 26th birthday or for 8 years from the last date of treatment, whichever is the longer.

IE-5.5 Financial records retention

Financial and billing records are retained for 7 years in accordance with Irish Revenue requirements under the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997.

IE-7. Liability — Ireland (Provider Model)

As Global Health operates in Ireland under the Provider Model, Global Health accepts institutional responsibility for the clinical governance framework under which Services are delivered. This means:

  • Global Health is responsible for verifying and maintaining the IMC registration status of all Clinicians delivering services to Irish patients through the Platform.
  • Global Health maintains professional indemnity cover appropriate to its role as the operating healthcare entity in Ireland.
  • Individual Clinicians also maintain their own professional indemnity insurance as required by their IMC registration obligations.
  • Liability for individual clinical decisions rests with the treating Clinician, who exercises independent professional judgment. Global Health's institutional liability relates to governance, systems, and oversight.

Nothing in this clause limits or excludes any liability that cannot be excluded under Irish law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

The limitation of liability provisions in Clause 11 of the Master Terms apply subject to Irish mandatory consumer and healthcare law.
