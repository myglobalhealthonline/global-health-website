# Irish citation & link targets — myglobalhealth.online

Generated 2026-08-04 alongside the OpenSEO live-data pass.

**Why this exists.** 57 referring domains, and the profile is almost entirely
legacy Wix short links plus article farms. Genuine Irish health citations
number **two**: `coombecommunitypharmacy.ie` and `askspud.ie` — and both
currently produce a broken link (DataForSEO flags 1 broken backlink each), so
fixing those two is the cheapest win on this page.

Every competitor beating you for `sick cert online` (smartscripts.ie,
getsickcert.ie, gpsicknote.ie, medhub.ie) is a thin single-purpose site. They
do not outrank you on content depth. They outrank you on topical trust signals,
and Irish citations are the cheapest of those to acquire.

All domains below were checked on 2026-08-04 and resolve.

## Tier 1 — insurer & payer directories (highest trust, hardest gate)

These are provider listings, not link schemes. They require an actual
commercial relationship, so treat them as business development that happens to
produce a citation.

| Domain | Route in |
|---|---|
| `vhi.ie` | Provider/partner listing — requires Vhi recognition for your services. |
| `layahealthcare.ie` | Same model; Laya's directory of approved providers. |
| `irishlifehealth.ie` | Same model. |

Your service pages already emit an `insuranceSeoLine` when companies cover a
service — so the data model for this is in place, which means the block is
commercial, not technical.

## Tier 2 — trade press (editorial, earns real authority)

Pitch a story, not a link. The angle that works for a telehealth clinic is
data: you have real numbers on sick-cert demand across six markets.

| Domain | Angle |
|---|---|
| `medicalindependent.ie` | Clinical/practice-facing. Telehealth adoption, GP capacity. |
| `irishpharmacynews.ie` | Pharmacy-adjacent — your prescription and home-delivery services. |
| `thejournal.ie` | Consumer. Sick-leave rights explainers are their format. |
| `independent.ie` | Consumer health desk. Hardest, highest value. |

## Tier 3 — business & local directories

| Domain | Notes |

## Tier 4 — professional / regulatory (citations, not links)

| Domain | Reality check |
|---|---|
| `icgp.ie` | Professional body. No public provider directory to list in — but your doctors' ICGP/IMC memberships are E-E-A-T signals worth naming on their profile pages. |
| `hse.ie` | Regulator-adjacent. Does not link to private providers. Listed here so nobody spends a week trying. |

## What not to do

- Do not buy directory placements. That is exactly what produced the 28
  domains in `disavow-2026-08-04.txt`.
- Do not pursue guest posts on the article farms already linking to you
  (`articlescad.com`, `articlewaves.com`, `freearticlesmania.com`,
  `blogsgod.com`). They are already in the profile and contribute nothing.
- Do not chase volume. Two `.ie` health citations are worth more here than
  fifty of what you currently have.

## Realistic expectation

Tier 0 is this week. Tier 3 is a day of form-filling. Tier 2 is a quarter of
relationship-building for maybe two placements. Tier 1 tracks your insurance
partnerships, not your SEO calendar. None of this moves `online gp ireland`
(KD 27) inside three months — it is what makes that move possible later.
